## Learning outcomes (CCVX syllabus)

> **The CCVX PDF prints the wrong text here.** Under D2 it repeats C10 (Activation Energy). The Dutch exam programme (CvTE syllabus 2027, Bijlage 1, eindterm 31) defines D2 as:
>
> *De kandidaat kan met behulp van kennis van eigenschappen van stoffen en materialen in experimenten deze stoffen of materialen analyseren en zuiveren en daarbij veilig omgaan met stoffen, materialen en apparatuur.*
>
> Unofficial translation: the candidate is able, using knowledge of the properties of substances and materials, to analyse and purify those substances or materials in experiments, handling substances, materials and equipment safely.

Text printed under D2 in the CCVX PDF (a duplicate of C10):

The candidate is able to use and describe the concept of activation energy in experiments and relate it to catalysis.
