## A1 Using information skills

- Information skills (*informatievaardigheden*) means looking for information with a clear goal, judging whether it is reliable and relevant, selecting what you need and working it into an answer or a report. *(SLO A1)*
- For a CCVX candidate it shows up in two ways. In the exam: picking the data you need out of a long context text, a table, a figure or Binas, which is practised in A08 — scientific instruments. While preparing: choosing trustworthy study sources, which uses the reliability checks in A09 — evaluation and judgment.
- The CvTE syllabus doesn't specify this skill for the Dutch central exam, and the CCVX syllabus leaves out aspects that can't be tested in a written exam. So it is low priority on its own: practise it through A08 and A09.
