# A13 — Reasoning about Systems, Change and Energy

- **In Dutch VWO:** skill, tested inside content questions in both the central exam (CE) and the school exam (SE).
- **Dutch source:** [CvTE 2027](../../syllabus/cvte-syllabus-scheikunde-vwo-2027.pdf) domain A → A13 (a new systems-thinking specification); its Bijlage 4 lists past-exam questions that test it.

## Learning outcomes (CCVX syllabus)

The candidate is able to describe chemical processes in terms of systems, using knowledge of substances, particles, reactivity and energy.
