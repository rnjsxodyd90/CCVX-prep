## A3 Reflecting on learning

- Reflecting on learning (*reflecteren op leren*) means looking at your own interests, motivation and way of studying, analysing how a task went and what you learned, and adjusting your approach so you learn more effectively. *(SLO A3)*
- The CvTE text prints the whole end term in grey italics: the Dutch central exam doesn't test it. A written exam such as CCVX can't test it either, so it is low priority.
- It is still useful while you prepare: after each practice exam, list your mistakes by type (units, significant figures, unbalanced equations, misread questions) and plan your revision around the most frequent ones.
