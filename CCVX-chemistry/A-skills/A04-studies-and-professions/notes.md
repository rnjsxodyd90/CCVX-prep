## A4 Studies and professions

- Studies and professions (*studie en beroep*) means describing how science is used in further studies and jobs, and on that basis putting your own interest in a study or profession into words. Dutch schools work on it through visits, masterclasses and orientation days at universities. *(SLO A4)*
- The CvTE text prints the whole end term in grey italics: the Dutch central exam doesn't test it. A written exam such as CCVX can't test it either, so it is low priority.
- For a CCVX candidate it is mostly the reason for taking the exam: CCVX results are used for admission to Dutch higher education, and chemistry is needed in studies such as medicine, pharmacy, chemical engineering and the life sciences.
