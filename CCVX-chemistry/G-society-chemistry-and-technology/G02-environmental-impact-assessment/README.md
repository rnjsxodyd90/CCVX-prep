# G2 — Environmental Impact Assessment

- **In Dutch VWO:** central exam (CE).
- **Dutch source:** [CvTE 2027](../../syllabus/cvte-syllabus-scheikunde-vwo-2027.pdf) T4 *Milieu en gezondheid* (T4.1); transport is in T5.1.

> Combined heat and power was dropped from the Dutch syllabus, but CCVX still lists it.

## Learning outcomes (CCVX syllabus)

The candidate is able at least in the context of health or sustainability to describe what social conditions play a role in environmental issues, using knowledge of production processes, and what potential consequences these issues have in the area of health and sustainability.

1. The candidate is able to describe what potential consequences a production process has for the environment and health, given data on that process:
    - transport of raw materials, products and waste
    - emissions
    - limits
    - use of cooling water
    - risks in the event of an emergency
    - combined heat and power
    - sustainability.
