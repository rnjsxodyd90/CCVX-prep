# C9 — Quality of Energy

- **In Dutch VWO:** school exam (SE) only, so it isn't in the CvTE syllabus. CCVX still tests it.
- **Dutch source:** [SLO handreiking SE (vwo)](https://www.slo.nl/handreikingen/havo-vwo/handreiking-se-schei-hv/toetsen-schoolexamen/vakinhoudelijke-0/) → C9. Overlap: [CvTE 2027](../../syllabus/cvte-syllabus-scheikunde-vwo-2027.pdf) R5.

## Learning outcomes (CCVX syllabus)

The candidate is able to describe how energy and the quality of energy changes in chemical processes, using knowledge of energy:

- energy transitions
- types of energy
- exothermic, endothermic
- energy diagram
- heat of formation, fossil fuel, biofuel
- thermodynamic concepts such as Gibbs energy and enthalpy.
