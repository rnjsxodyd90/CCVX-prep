# sb12–sb16 — Reactions

- **In Dutch VWO:** assumed prior knowledge from lower secondary school.
- **Dutch source:** Lower-secondary material, e.g. [Wikiwijs Stercollectie Scheikunde hv3](https://maken.wikiwijs.nl/82750/Scheikunde_hv3). In [CvTE 2027](../../syllabus/cvte-syllabus-scheikunde-vwo-2027.pdf): R1.1–R1.3, R5.1, R&A2.1 (*reagens*) and R&A3.1.

## Learning outcomes (CCVX syllabus)

The candidate is able to:

- **sb12** describe what is meant by a chemical reaction:
    - reactants
    - reaction products
    - law of conservation of elements
    - law of mass conservation
- **sb13** describe what is meant by a decomposition reaction:
    - thermolysis
    - electrolysis
    - photolysis
- **sb14** describe what is meant by a combustion reaction:
    - complete and incomplete combustion
    - ignition temperature
- **sb15** describe that chemical reactions are associated with a heat effect:
    - exothermic
    - endothermic
- **sb16** describe how various substances are detected:
    - reagent
    - oxygen, hydrogen, water, carbon dioxide, sulfur dioxide.
