# F4 — Risks and Safety

- **In Dutch VWO:** school exam (SE) only, so it isn't in the CvTE syllabus. CCVX still tests it.
- **Dutch source:** [SLO handreiking SE (vwo)](https://www.slo.nl/handreikingen/havo-vwo/handreiking-se-schei-hv/toetsen-schoolexamen/vakinhoudelijke-0/) → F4.

> R and S phrases have since been replaced by H and P statements (GHS/CLP); know both.

## Learning outcomes (CCVX syllabus)

The candidate is able to use knowledge of risks and safety and judge those aspects in industrial production processes:

- substance flows, energy flows, risk and probability, change of scale (on a large scale), risk (R) and safety (S) sentences, pictograms, substance cycles.
