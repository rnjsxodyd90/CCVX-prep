# CCVX Chemistry

Study material for the [CCVX](https://www.ccvx.nl/index%20-%20EN.html) entrance exam (*voortentamen*) in chemistry, organised by the CCVX syllabus in `syllabus/ccvx-syllabus-chemistry-EN.pdf` (dated 20 September 2020). The exam covers the whole Dutch VWO chemistry programme, both the central-exam and the school-exam parts, except what can't be tested in writing. Where the CCVX syllabus and the current Dutch syllabus differ, the CCVX syllabus applies.

## The exam

- Written, 3 hours, in Dutch or English, in Utrecht only. Registration closes 10 days before the exam; the fee is €98.
- Dates in 2026/27: Thursday 26 November 2026 (13:30–16:30), around 12 April 2027 and around 16 July 2027.
- Allowed: Binas, 5th edition or later, with no notes in it (the English edition, ISBN 978 90 01 70731 6, is a translation of the 5th); a non-graphing calculator with exponential and logarithmic functions; a paper dictionary.
- CCVX questions lean more on calculation and less on reading than the Dutch VWO exams do.

## Layout

There is one folder per syllabus subdomain. Each folder's `README.md` has the CCVX learning outcomes (the PDF's text, with formula formatting and obvious typos fixed), whether the subdomain belongs to the Dutch central exam or school exam, and where it sits in the Dutch syllabus.

| Folder | Syllabus | In Dutch VWO |
|---|---|---|
| `0-basic-knowledge/` | sb1–sb18 | prior knowledge from lower secondary |
| `A-skills/` | A1–A15 | tested inside content questions; A1–A4 can't be tested in writing |
| `B-substances-and-materials/` | B1–B4 | central exam |
| `C-chemical-processes-and-conservation-laws/` | C1–C10 | C1–C6 central exam; C7–C10 school exam |
| `D-development-of-chemical-knowledge/` | D1–D4 | D1, D3 central exam; D2, D4 school exam |
| `E-innovation-and-chemical-research/` | E1–E5 | E1, E2 central exam; E3–E5 school exam |
| `F-industrial-chemical-processes/` | F1–F5 | F1–F3 central exam; F4, F5 school exam |
| `G-society-chemistry-and-technology/` | G1–G5 | G1–G3 central exam; G4, G5 school exam |
| `syllabus/` | reference PDFs | |

Subfolder numbers are zero-padded so they sort correctly: `A01` is syllabus A1.
