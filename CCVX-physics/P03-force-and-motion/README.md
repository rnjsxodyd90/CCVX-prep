# P3 — Force and motion

- **Dutch:** *kracht en beweging*
- **CCVX syllabus:** subdomain C1 — *Kracht en beweging* (plus the assumed knowledge ρ = m/V). **Syllabus version used: 15 August 2025 (in force from 1 September 2025).**

## Learning outcomes (CCVX syllabus)

The candidate can:

1. Make calculations on uniform rectilinear motions (*eenparige rechtlijnige bewegingen*): x(t) = v·t with v constant; v_gem = Δx/Δt.
2. Determine properties of motions from position–time diagrams (*plaats-tijddiagrammen*, (x,t)) and velocity–time diagrams (*snelheidtijddiagrammen*, (v,t)): recognise a uniform motion, a uniformly accelerated/decelerated motion (*eenparig versnelde/vertraagde beweging*), free fall (*vrije val*) and a fall with friction (*valbeweging met wrijving*); determine the average velocity from an (x,t) diagram; determine the velocity at an instant from an (x,t) diagram using that the velocity is the derivative of position, v = dx/dt (tangent line); determine the acceleration at an instant from a (v,t) diagram using a = dv/dt; determine displacement and average velocity from a (v,t) diagram with the area; use x(t) = ½at² and v(t) = at for constant a from rest.
3. Analyse forces on a system both with a vector drawing and with goniometric relations, including composing forces and resolving them into components, and determining magnitude and/or direction: gravity (*zwaartekracht* F_z = m·g), sliding friction (*schuifwrijvingskracht* F_w,s,max = f·F_N), rolling resistance (*rolweerstandskracht*), air resistance (*luchtweerstandskracht* F_w,l = ½ρc_wAv²), normal force (*normaalkracht*), tension (*spankracht*), muscle force (*spierkracht*), spring force (*veerkracht* F_v = C·u).
4. Explain and apply Newton's first law: the concept inertia (*traagheid*) — a body on which the resultant force is zero keeps its velocity (including rest).
5. Explain and apply Newton's second law: F⃗_res = ΣF⃗_i = m·a⃗.
6. Explain and apply Newton's third law: action force (*actiekracht*) and reaction force (*reactiekracht*), F⃗_AB = −F⃗_BA, acting on different bodies; the concept weight (*gewicht*): the force a body exerts on its support or suspension.
