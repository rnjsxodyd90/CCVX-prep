# P5 — Gravitation

- **Dutch:** *gravitatie*
- **CCVX syllabus:** subdomain C3 — *Gravitatie* (plus the assumed knowledge: the build-up of our solar system — sun, moon and planets — and the concept energy storage). **Syllabus version used: 15 August 2025 (in force from 1 September 2025).**

## Learning outcomes (CCVX syllabus)

The candidate can:

1. Analyse circular motions with constant orbital speed (*cirkelbewegingen met constante baansnelheid*): the concepts orbital period (*omlooptijd*) T, orbital radius (*baanstraal*) r and orbital speed (*baansnelheid*) v = 2πr/T; make calculations on the centripetal force (*middelpuntzoekende kracht*) F_mpz = mv²/r in situations where a single force plays the role of centripetal force (in constructions, drawings and reasoning several forces can jointly fulfil this role).
2. Analyse motions of objects in a gravitational field using the gravitational force **F_g = G·mM/r²** and the gravitational energy **E_g = −G·mM/r**, with the gravitational constant G = 6.674·10⁻¹¹ N·m²/kg² (BINAS):
   - analyse the motions of planets, comets and other celestial bodies with a numerical model (conceptually);
   - apply the relation between the escape velocity (*ontsnappingssnelheid*) and the mass and radius of a celestial body;
   - explain how the gravitational acceleration at a planet's surface follows from the mass and radius of the planet;
   - the concepts: gravitational interaction (*gravitatiewisselwerking*), elliptical orbit (*ellipsbaan*), geostationary orbit (*geostationaire baan*);
   - at minimum in the contexts: moon (*maan*), planet (*planeet*), satellite (*satelliet*).
