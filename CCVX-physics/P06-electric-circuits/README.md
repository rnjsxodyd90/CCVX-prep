# P6 — Electric circuits

- **Dutch:** *elektrische schakelingen*
- **CCVX syllabus:** subdomain D1 — *Elektrische systemen* (plus the assumed knowledge: drawing and interpreting circuit diagrams; the concepts conductor — *geleider* — and insulator — *isolator*). **Syllabus version used: 15 August 2025 (in force from 1 September 2025)** — note: the parallel-resistance formula in this version reads **1/R_tot = 1/R₁ + 1/R₂ + …** (changed from the pre-September-2025 list).

## Learning outcomes (CCVX syllabus)

The candidate can:

1. Explain the phenomenon of electric current as displacement of charge due to an applied voltage: use the definitions of current (*stroomsterkte*) **I = Q/t** (A = C/s), voltage (*spanning*) **U = ΔE/Q** (V = J/C) and resistivity (*soortelijke weerstand*) **ρ = RA/ℓ**; the concepts: free electron (*vrij elektron*), ion (*ion*), elementary charge (*elementaire lading* e = 1.602·10⁻¹⁹ C, BINAS), voltage source (*spanningsbron*), battery (*batterij*), accumulator (*accu*).
2. Analyse circuits and make calculations with Ohm's law **U = I·R** and the conductance (*geleidbaarheid*) **G = 1/R**:
   - series circuit (*serieschakeling*): U_tot = U₁ + U₂ + …, I_tot = I₁ = I₂ = …, R_tot = R₁ + R₂ + … — voltage division (*spanningsdeling*);
   - parallel circuit (*parallelschakeling*): U_tot = U₁ = U₂ = …, I_tot = I₁ + I₂ + …, **1/R_tot = 1/R₁ + 1/R₂ + …** — current division (*stroomdeling*);
   - reason and calculate with mixed circuits (*gemengde schakelingen*);
   - connect an ammeter (in series, ideally zero resistance) and a voltmeter (in parallel, ideally infinite resistance) correctly;
   - apply these components inside a circuit: diode, LDR, NTC, PTC, ohmic resistor (*ohmse weerstand*), lamp, motor, heating element (*verwarmingselement*), fuse (*zekering*), residual-current device (*aardlekschakelaar*);
   - the concept short circuit (*kortsluiting*);
   - Kirchhoff's laws limited to their form for series and parallel circuits (no general formulation needed).
3. Analyse the power and efficiency of energy conversions in an electric circuit: **P = U·I** (and P = I²R = U²/R), energy **E = P·t**, electrical energy in joule and in kilowatt-hour (1 kWh = 3.6·10⁶ J), efficiency η = E_nuttig/E_in = P_nuttig/P_in — at minimum in the contexts: light sources and household appliances (*gloeilamp*, *spaarlamp*, LED, *elektromotor*, *verwarmingselement* and the kWh meter), energy use, energy saving, and the generation of electricity (*opwekking van elektriciteit*).
