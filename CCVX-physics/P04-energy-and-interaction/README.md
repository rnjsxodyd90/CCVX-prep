# P4 — Energy and interaction

- **Dutch:** *energie en wisselwerking*
- **CCVX syllabus:** subdomain C2 — *Energie en wisselwerking*. **Syllabus version used: 15 August 2025 (in force from 1 September 2025).**

## Learning outcomes (CCVX syllabus)

The candidate can:

1. Make calculations on force, displacement, work, speed and power: work *W = F·s·cos α* with the sign of the work (positive and negative work — *positieve en negatieve arbeid*, friction work *wrijvingsarbeid*); determine work from a force–displacement diagram (*kracht-verplaatsingsdiagram*) as an area; power **P = ΔE/t = W/t = F·v**.
2. Analyse energy conversions using the concepts conservation of energy (*energiebehoud*), efficiency (*rendement*), work (*arbeid*) and heat (*warmte*):
   - apply the law of conservation of energy **ΣE_in = ΣE_uit** and the work–kinetic-energy relation **ΣW = ΔE_k**;
   - at minimum for the motions: free fall, fall with friction, vertical throw (*verticale worp*), oscillation (*trilling*) and bouncing motion (*stuiterbeweging*) — all periodic motion (*periodieke beweging*);
   - the energies: kinetic energy **E_k = ½mv²**, gravitational energy (*zwaarte-energie*) **E_z = mgh**, spring energy (*veerenergie*) **E_veer = ½Cu²**, chemical energy **E_ch = r_v·V** and **E_ch = r_m·m** (calorific values *stookwaarden* from BINAS), electric energy, radiation energy, heat;
   - the concept potential energy (*potentiële energie*);
   - compute the efficiency **η = E_nuttig/E_in = P_nuttig/P_in**;
   - at minimum in the contexts: energy use and energy saving in traffic (*energiegebruik en energiebesparing in het verkeer*) and the moving human (*de bewegende mens*).
