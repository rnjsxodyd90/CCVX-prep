# P9 — Optics

- **Dutch:** *(geometrische) optica: breking en spiegeling*
- **CCVX syllabus:** subdomain E1 — *Eigenschappen van stoffen en materialen*, the optics block (specifications 11–14). **Syllabus version used: 15 August 2025 (in force from 1 September 2025).**

## Learning outcomes (CCVX syllabus)

The candidate knows, from the assumed knowledge (*bekend verondersteld*): divergent, convergent and parallel beams of light (*divergente, convergente en evenwijdige lichtbundels*); shadow formation (*schaduwvorming*); mirror reflection and diffuse reflection (*spiegelende en diffuse terugkaatsing*); the concepts frequency, wavelength, wave speed, interference, diffraction (*buiging*), grating (*tralie*), standing waves; geometric optics: refraction (*breking*), mirroring (*spiegelen*), refractive index (*brekingsindex*), critical angle (*grenshoek*), dispersion (*dispersie*).

The candidate can:

1. Make calculations with the laws of refraction and draw the path of light (*de lichtweg tekenen*): angle of incidence (*hoek van inval*) i and angle of refraction (*hoek van breking*) r measured **to the normal**; the reflection law (*terugkaatsingswet*) **i = t** (angle of incidence = angle of reflection, measured to the normal); the refraction law (*brekingswet*, Snell's law) **sin i / sin r = n** for light passing from (near-)vacuum into a medium with refractive index n, and conversely sin r/sin i = n when it leaves; the critical angle (*grenshoek*) **sin g = 1/n**: for angles larger than g inside a medium, all light is totally internally reflected. Relate colour to wavelength with **f = c/λ**.
2. Use the refraction laws to describe how a glass fibre (*glasvezelkabel*) guides light (repeated total internal reflection) and how a prism produces colour dispersion (*kleurschifting*, because n differs slightly per colour: violet refracts strongest).
3. Indicate which techniques and principles are used to record and to transmit image and sound: digital technology (sampling and quantising), magnetic tape (*magneetband*) and compact disc (CD: pits read by a laser).
4. Give examples of applications of ultrasound and laser light in health care: ultrasound imaging (*echografie*); fibre-optic technology (*glasvezeltechniek*, endoscopy); and know the contexts: photo camera, telescope, projector, microscope, the human eye, and the laser.
