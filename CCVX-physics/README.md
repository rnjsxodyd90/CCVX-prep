# CCVX Physics

Study material for the [CCVX](https://www.ccvx.nl/index%20-%20EN.html) entrance exam (*voortentamen*) in Physics (*natuurkunde*), organised by the official CCVX syllabus: [Leerstof voortentamen natuurkunde](syllabus/leerstof-natuurkunde.pdf). **Syllabus version used: the document dated 15 August 2025, in force from 1 September 2025** — the CCVX announced this change (*"wijziging leerstof van natuurkunde"*, [notice](https://www.ccvx.nl/Inschrijving/Natuurkunde%20-%20aanpassing%20leerstof.html)): revised quantum mechanics (subdomain F1), a changed parallel-circuit formula, `E = mc²` added, and the Doppler effect added to B1. The new syllabus has been examined from the November 2025 sitting onwards; the next sitting is Tuesday 24 November 2026. Every folder README repeats the version it is built from. See [syllabus/](syllabus/) for the PDF and an extraction.

## The exam

- Written exam with open questions, 3 hours (13:30–16:30), in Dutch or English, in Utrecht only (Educatorium, halls Alfa and Gamma). Partial credit is given for correct steps, so always show your working.
- Dates in 2026/27: **Tuesday 24 November 2026**, around Wednesday 21 April 2027 and around Monday 19 July 2027. Registration closes 10 days before the exam; the fee is €98 per attempt.
- A **BINAS** booklet (5th edition or later; an English edition is allowed) may be used during the exam. Many syllabus formulas are in BINAS — but any syllabus formula that is not in BINAS must be known by heart, says the syllabus itself.
- Compared with the vwo central exam, CCVX puts more emphasis on doing calculations and less on reading-heavy context. Questions can combine several domains.
- Bring (per the official CCVX [exam requirements](https://www.ccvx.nl/Tentamenbenodigdheden%20en%20Handelingswerkwoorden.html)): valid ID; a pen (not red) and a pencil (pencil only for drawing graphs); a set square (*geodriehoek*); one simple scientific calculator with exponential, logarithmic and goniometric functions — **no graphing calculator**; and a watch (**no smartwatch**; a phone is not allowed as a clock). BINAS (5th edition or later) is required for physics and must contain no notes at all. A single-volume dictionary (Dutch or Dutch-to-your-language, not digital) is allowed. The same page lists the CCVX's official *action verbs* (calculate, explain, sketch, …) and links its [significant-figures rules](https://www.ccvx.nl/Significante%20cijfers.pdf).
- Free official sample exams (CCVX's own material, external): the CCVX [sample exam page](https://www.ccvx.nl/VoorbeeldTentamens/VoorbeeldTentamensNatuurkunde/VoorbeeldTentamensNatuurkunde.html).

## Layout

There is one folder per syllabus subdomain. Each folder has:

- a `README.md` with the learning outcomes in English, with the Dutch terms of the syllabus in brackets, and the syllabus version it follows;
- a `notes.md` with English explanations of every outcome, with worked examples at the end;
- a `practice.md` with 8–15 original practice questions, each with a full worked solution. Every question is original material written for this prep: nothing is copied from CCVX or CvTE exams.

The folders follow the content subdomains of the syllabus (domains B–F). The skills of domain A (*vaardigheden*, such as significant figures, graphs and model thinking) and the cross-cutting domains H (*natuurwetten en modellen*) and I (*onderzoek en ontwerp*) are trained inside every folder, as they are in the exam itself.
