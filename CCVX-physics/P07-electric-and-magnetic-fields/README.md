# P7 — Electric and magnetic fields

- **Dutch:** *elektrische en magnetische velden*
- **CCVX syllabus:** subdomain D2 — *Elektrische en magnetische velden*. **Syllabus version used: 15 August 2025 (in force from 1 September 2025).**

## Learning outcomes (CCVX syllabus)

The candidate can:

1. Describe an electric field as the consequence of the presence of electric charge: the Coulomb force **F_el = f·qQ/r²** (f = 8.99·10⁹ N·m²/C², BINAS); repulsive and attractive electric force (*afstotende en aantrekkende elektrische kracht*); homogeneous and radial electric fields, the field line (*veldlijn*); the field strength relation **E⃗ = ΔU/Δx** (magnitude E = U/d for a homogeneous field) and the force relation, printed in the syllabus as the vector equation F⃗_el = −q·E⃗ (use magnitudes F_el = q·E with the physical direction rule: field lines run from + to −, and a positive charge is pushed along the field direction).
2. Apply the relation between voltage and kinetic energy to a charged particle in a homogeneous electric field: **ΔE_el = q·U** and **ΔE_k = −ΔE_el**; use electric energy as a form of potential energy; explain the unit electronvolt (*elektronvolt*, 1 eV = 1.602·10⁻¹⁹ J); at minimum in the contexts: X-ray tube (*röntgenbuis*), linear accelerator (*lineaire versneller*).
3. Describe a magnetic field as the consequence of the presence of moving electric charge, and determine the direction of a magnetic field at a permanent magnet, a straight current-carrying wire and a coil: homogeneous and inhomogeneous magnetic field, field line, electromagnet (*elektromagneet*); the Earth's magnetic field (*aardmagnetisch veld*); apply the right-hand rule for the field around a wire and for the poles of a coil.
4. Describe the effect of a magnetic field on an electric current and on moving charge: magnitude and direction of the Lorentz force (*lorentzkracht*) **F_L = B·I·ℓ** (current-carrying wire, direction with the left-hand rule) and **F_L = B·q·v** (single moving charge); at minimum in the contexts: electric motor (*elektromotor*), loudspeaker (*luidspreker*).
5. Analyse electromagnetic induction phenomena in various situations: use the definition of flux (*flux*) **Φ = B⊥·A**; apply the insight that the induced voltage (*inductiespanning*) is directly proportional to the number of turns and to the flux change per unit time, **U_ind ∝ N·dΦ/dt**; at minimum in the situations: a moving magnet in a coil and a rotating wire frame in a homogeneous magnetic field; at minimum in the contexts: dynamo, microphone (*microfoon*).
