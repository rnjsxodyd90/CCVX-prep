# P10 — Electromagnetic radiation and stars

- **Dutch:** *elektromagnetische straling en materie*
- **CCVX syllabus:** subdomain E2 — *Elektromagnetische straling en materie*. **Syllabus version used: 15 August 2025 (in force from 1 September 2025)** — this version added **E = mc²** (the mass defect of stars, specification 3).

## Learning outcomes (CCVX syllabus)

The candidate can:

1. For bound electrons, determine the wavelengths and frequencies of spectral lines from energy-level diagrams (*energieniveauschema's*): **E_f = hf**, **E_f = hc/λ**, **E_f = |E_m − E_n|**; explain absorption and emission spectra; the concepts: photon (*foton*), ground state (*grondtoestand*), excited state (*aangeslagen toestand*), ionisation energy (*ionisatie-energie*); at minimum in the context of atoms.
2. Analyse the light of stars: use a Hertzsprung–Russell diagram (*hertzsprung-russelldiagram*) to classify stars by temperature, total radiated power and size; analyse the radial velocity of stars from the spectrum with **v = (Δλ/λ)·c**; conclude the presence of elements in stars from the spectrum; the concepts: Fraunhofer line (*fraunhoferlijn*), redshift and blueshift (*roodverschuiving en blauwverschuiving*).
3. Name and apply the fact that nuclear fusion of hydrogen is the main energy source of stars, with the concept mass defect (*massadefect*) and **E = mc²** (with m the mass difference in kg, c = 3.00·10⁸ m/s).
4. Describe and apply the relation between the emitted wavelengths and temperature: apply Wien's law (*wet van Wien*) **λ_max·T = k_W** (k_W = 2.898·10⁻³ m·K, BINAS); the concepts Planck curve (*planck-kromme*) and continuous spectrum (*continu spectrum*); at minimum in the contexts: incandescent lamps (*gloeilampen*) and stars.
5. Explain how the observed intensity of a star on Earth follows from the star's total radiated power and its distance, and apply the Stefan–Boltzmann law (*wet van Stefan-Boltzmann*): **I = P_bron/(4πr²)** and **P_bron = σ·A·T⁴** (σ = 5.670·10⁻⁸ W/(m²·K⁴), BINAS); at minimum in the context of the Sun (*zon*).
6. Describe, from suitable tables and other sources, the different parts of the electromagnetic spectrum and the properties of these kinds of radiation: gamma radiation, X-radiation, ultraviolet, (visible) light, infrared, radio waves, microwaves; the instruments: optical telescope, radio telescope, space telescope.
