# Mock exam 2 — CCVX Mathematics B (practice)

Original practice exam written for this prep. Nothing is copied from CCVX or CvTE exams.

- Time: 180 minutes. Total: 76 points over 4 problems.
- Grade: 1 + 9 × (points scored)/76, rounded to one decimal.
- Work algebraically and exactly. Give exact answers (forms with √, π, e, ln) unless the question asks for a rounding.
- No graphing calculator. The formula sheet printed below is the one from the real CCVX wiskunde B exam. A protractor/ruler and a standard scientific calculator are allowed.
- Marking scheme: [mock-exam-2-marking-scheme.md](mock-exam-2-marking-scheme.md).

## Formula sheet

sin²(x) + cos²(x) = 1

sin(t + u) = sin t cos u + cos t sin u
sin(t − u) = sin t cos u − cos t sin u
cos(t + u) = cos t cos u − sin t sin u
cos(t − u) = cos t cos u + sin t sin u

sin(2t) = 2 sin t cos t
cos(2t) = cos²t − sin²t = 2 cos²t − 1 = 1 − 2 sin²t

## Problem 1 — The triangular roof (18 points)

A gable roof sits on a house 8.0 m wide. Take coordinates with the origin at the left wall base (x, y in metres). The roof edges run from P(0, 2) to the ridge R(4, 3) and from R to Q(8, 2).

a. (2; mathb-mock2-q01) Show that triangle PQR is isosceles.

b. (4; mathb-mock2-q02) Compute the angle at R in triangle PQR, using the dot product (1 decimal°).

c. (4; mathb-mock2-q03) Give an equation of the circumscribed circle of triangle PQR. (Hint: use the perpendicular bisectors.)

d. (4; mathb-mock2-q04) A horizontal roof window at height y = 2.4 spans the full width between the two roof edges PR and RQ. Compute the exact width of the window.

e. (4; mathb-mock2-q05) Compute the exact area of triangle PQR in two ways: once with ½ × base × height, and once with ½·|PR|·|QR|·sin(angle at R). Show that both give the same exact value.

## Problem 2 — Harbour tide (20 points)

In a harbour the water level during 24 hours is modelled by the sinusoid

h(t) = 3.0 + 0.8·cos(π/6·(t − 10)),   0 ≤ t ≤ 24 (t in hours after midnight, h in metres).

a. (2; mathb-mock2-q06) Give the water level at high water (maximum) and at low water (minimum), with the times of the first high and the first low after midnight.

b. (3; mathb-mock2-q07) Compute exactly the first time after midnight at which the level crosses the equilibrium level while falling.

c. (4; mathb-mock2-q08) A ship needs a water level of at least 3.4 m. Compute the first window of positive length (from → to) after midnight during which the ship can enter, exactly.

d. (3; mathb-mock2-q09) Compute the average rate of change of the water level between t = 0 and t = 6, and the instantaneous rate dh/dt at t = 6. Give both exactly, with units.

e. (4; mathb-mock2-q10) Solve exactly on [0, 24]: h(t) = 3.4. (You may build on part c, but write the complete solution.)

f. (4; mathb-mock2-q11) The harbour master replaces the model between t = 6 and t = 12 by the straight line through the model's points at t = 6 and t = 12. By what exact amount does his approximation deviate from the model at t = 9?

## Problem 3 — The logarithmic family (18 points)

The functions f_p are given by f_p(x) = ln(x) − ln(x − p), with x > p and parameter p > 0.

a. (2; mathb-mock2-q12) Show that f_p(x) = ln(x/(x − p)).

b. (3; mathb-mock2-q13) Show with limit calculations that the graph of f_p has the line x = p as a vertical asymptote and the line y = 0 as a horizontal asymptote.

c. (4; mathb-mock2-q14) Show with the derivative that f_p is decreasing on its whole domain.

d. (4; mathb-mock2-q15) For which x is f₂(x) = 1? Give an exact answer.

e. (5; mathb-mock2-q16) Take g(x) = ln(x) − ln(2x − 3) for x > 3/2. Compute exactly the total area of the region(s) bounded by the graph of g, the x-axis and the lines x = 2 and x = 4. (Tip: compute where g(x) = 0 first, and use the standard antiderivative of ln.)

## Problem 4 — Parabolas with a parameter (20 points)

For each value of p, the function f_p is given by f_p(x) = −x² + px.

a. (2; mathb-mock2-q17) Compute the x-values where the graph of f_p meets the x-axis.

b. (4; mathb-mock2-q18) Show that the vertex of the parabola is (p/2, p²/4), and give an equation of the curve on which all these vertices lie.

c. (4; mathb-mock2-q19) For which values of p does the line y = −2x + 4 touch the parabola of f_p? Give the touching points as well.

d. (5; mathb-mock2-q20) Take p = 4. Compute exactly the area of the region enclosed by the graph of f₄ and the x-axis.

e. (5; mathb-mock2-q21) The region of part d rotates about the x-axis. Compute the exact volume of the resulting solid.
