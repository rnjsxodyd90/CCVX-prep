# Syllabus documents

Source: [CCVX](https://www.ccvx.nl/), Mathematics section.

- [leerstof-ccvw-wiskunde-b-2018.pdf](leerstof-ccvw-wiskunde-b-2018.pdf): the official CCVX Mathematics B syllabus (*Leerstof CCVW wiskunde B*, in force since December 2018). Direct link: <https://www.ccvx.nl/Leerstof%20CCVW%20wiskunde%20B%202018.pdf>
- [leerstof-wiskunde-b.nl.txt](leerstof-wiskunde-b.nl.txt): the Dutch text extracted from the PDF.
- [leerstof-wiskunde-b.en.md](leerstof-wiskunde-b.en.md): an English translation of the exam programme and the concept list, made for this prep.

The syllabus PDF says the exam programme is based on the Dutch VWO exam programme wiskunde B for 2019, with one important difference: no graphing calculator or other ICT is allowed. Recommended study books named in the syllabus: Getal en Ruimte vwo B (11th or 12th edition, parts 1–4, all chapters except part 3 chapter K), or equivalent VWO methods; Wiswijs as preparation.

The free official CCVX example exams for mathematics are linked from <https://www.ccvx.nl/> under *Voorbeeldexamens*. They are not copied into this folder.
