# B11 — Triangle geometry

- **Dutch:** *meetkunde in driehoeken*
- **CCVX syllabus:** programme point 13 and the section *meetkunde in driehoeken* of the concept list.

## Learning outcomes (CCVX syllabus)

The candidate can investigate and prove geometric properties of triangles using geometric and algebraic techniques. In detail:

1. Apply the Pythagorean theorem (*stelling van Pythagoras*).
2. Compute the area of a triangle: ½ × base × height (*oppervlakte van een driehoek*).
3. Use the angle sum (*hoekensom*): the angles of a triangle add to 180°.
4. Apply the sine rule (*sinusregel*) a/sin α = b/sin β = c/sin γ and the cosine rule (*cosinusregel*) a² = b² + c² − 2bc·cos α.
5. Use isosceles triangles (*gelijkbenige driehoeken*): the base angles are equal; equilateral triangles (*gelijkzijdige driehoeken*): all angles are 60°.
6. Use similar triangles (*gelijkvormige driehoeken*): equal angles give equal ratios of corresponding sides, and conversely.
7. Use medians (*zwaartelijnen*) and the centroid (*zwaartepunt*): the centroid divides each median in the ratio 2 : 1.
8. Prove geometric properties, combining these results with coordinates, vectors and the circle theorems of [B13](../B13-circle-geometry/).
