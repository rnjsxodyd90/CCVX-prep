# CCVX Mathematics B

Study material for the [CCVX](https://www.ccvx.nl/index%20-%20EN.html) entrance exam (*voortentamen*) in Mathematics B, organised by the official CCVX syllabus: [Leerstof CCVW wiskunde B 2018](syllabus/leerstof-ccvw-wiskunde-b-2018.pdf) (in force since December 2018, unchanged for 2026/2027). An [English text version](syllabus/leerstof-wiskunde-b.en.md) of the syllabus is included for reference.

## The exam

- Written exam with open questions, 3 hours, in Dutch or English, in Utrecht only. Registration closes 10 days before the exam; the fee is €98 per attempt.
- Dates in 2026/27: Tuesday 15 December 2026 (13:30–16:30), around 19 April 2027 and around 23 July 2027.
- No graphing calculator, and no calculator that can compute integrals. A standard scientific calculator (Casio fx-82 or TI-30 class), with exponential, logarithmic and trigonometric functions, is allowed.
- All computations must be algebraic or exact: exact values such as √3, π/6 and e² appear in questions and answers.
- A formula sheet (the Pythagorean identity and the angle sum, difference and double-angle formulas) is printed on the last page of the exam.
- Bring: valid ID, a pen (pencil only for drawing graphs), a ruler or set square (*geodriehoek*), a watch (no smartwatch), food and drink.

## Layout

There is one folder per syllabus subdomain. Each folder has:

- a `README.md` with the learning outcomes in English, with the Dutch terms of the syllabus in brackets;
- a `notes.md` with English explanations of every outcome, with worked examples inline at the end;
- a `practice.md` with 8–15 original practice questions, each with a full worked solution. Every question is original material written for this prep: nothing is copied from CCVX or CvTE exams.
