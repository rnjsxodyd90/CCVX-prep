# B5 — Trigonometry

- **Dutch:** *goniometrie*
- **CCVX syllabus:** programme points 4 and 12, and the section *goniometrie* of the concept list.

## Learning outcomes (CCVX syllabus)

The candidate can:

1. Use sine, cosine and tangent in a right-angled triangle (*rechthoekige driehoek*): SOH-CAH-TOA — sin = opposite/hypotenuse, cos = adjacent/hypotenuse, tan = opposite/adjacent — and tan(x) = sin(x)/cos(x).
2. Read angles on the unit circle (*eenheidscirkel*), and convert between degrees and radians (*radialen*): 180° = π rad, 1° = π/180 rad. For goniometric functions we almost always work in radians.
3. Use the exact values of sine and cosine of the standard angles 0, π/6 (30°), π/4 (45°), π/3 (60°), π/2 (90°) from the 30-60-90 and 45-45-90 triangles, and extend them across the unit circle with symmetry.
4. Use the Pythagorean identity (*stelling van Pythagoras*) cos²α + sin²α = 1.
5. Apply the sum and difference formulas (*somformules*, printed on the exam's formula sheet):
   - sin(t + u) = sin t cos u + cos t sin u;
   - sin(t − u) = sin t cos u − cos t sin u;
   - cos(t + u) = cos t cos u − sin t sin u;
   - cos(t − u) = cos t cos u + sin t sin u;
   and their consequences sin(2t) = 2 sin t cos t and cos(2t) = cos²t − sin²t = 2 cos²t − 1 = 1 − 2 sin²t.
6. Compute sides and angles of arbitrary triangles with the sine rule (*sinusregel*) a/sin α = b/sin β = c/sin γ and the cosine rule (*cosinusregel*) a² = b² + c² − 2bc cos α (see also [B11](../B11-triangle-geometry/)).
