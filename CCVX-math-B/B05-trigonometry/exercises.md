# B5 — Practice questions with solutions

Ten original practice questions, written for this prep. Nothing is copied from CCVX or CvTE exams. Compute exactly where possible; a full worked solution follows every question.

## Q1 — degrees and radians

a. Convert 330° to radians exactly.

b. Convert 4π/5 rad to degrees.

**Solution.** a. 330 × π/180 = 11π/6 rad. b. (4π/5) × (180/π)° = 4 × 36° = 144°.

**Answer:** a. 11π/6; b. 144°

## Q2 — exact values on the unit circle

Give the exact values: sin(7π/6), cos(5π/4), tan(2π/3).

**Solution.** 7π/6 = π + π/6 (quadrant III): sin = −sin(π/6) = −½. 5π/4 = π + π/4 (quadrant III): cos = −cos(π/4) = −½√2. 2π/3 = π − π/3 (quadrant II): sin(2π/3) = ½√3 and cos(2π/3) = −½, so tan = (½√3)/(−½) = −√3.

**Answer:** −½, −½√2, −√3

## Q3 — right triangle

In right-angled triangle ABC (right angle at C): angle A = 50°, side AC = 6. Compute AB and BC (2 decimals).

**Solution.** AC is adjacent to A, AB the hypotenuse: cos 50° = 6/AB ⇔ AB = 6/cos 50° = 6/0.6428 ≈ 9.33. BC is opposite: BC = 6·tan 50° = 6 × 1.1918 ≈ 7.15.

**Answer:** AB ≈ 9.33, BC ≈ 7.15

## Q4 — from sin to cos and tan

Given sin α = 2/3 with 0 < α < ½π. Compute cos α and tan α exactly.

**Solution.** cos²α = 1 − 4/9 = 5/9, quadrant I so cos α = √5/3 (positive). tan α = (2/3)/(√5/3) = 2/√5 = 2√5/5 ≈ 0.894.

**Answer:** cos α = √5/3, tan α = 2√5/5 ≈ 0.894

## Q5 — exact value via a difference formula

Compute cos(15°) exactly.

**Solution.** cos(45° − 30°) = cos 45° cos 30° + sin 45° sin 30° = ½√2 · ½√3 + ½√2 · ½ = ¼√6 + ¼√2 = ¼(√6 + √2) ≈ 0.966.

**Answer:** ¼(√6 + √2) ≈ 0.966

## Q6 — recognising identities

Write as one goniometric term: (a) sin²(3x) + cos²(3x); (b) 1 − 2 sin²(x); (c) 2 sin(x) cos(x).

**Solution.** (a) 1 (Pythagorean identity, argument 3x). (b) cos(2x) (formula sheet: cos 2t = 1 − 2 sin²t). (c) sin(2x).

**Answer:** (a) 1; (b) cos(2x); (c) sin(2x)

## Q7 — sine rule

In triangle ABC: a = 12, α = 40°, β = 75°. Compute b (2 decimals) and angle γ.

**Solution.** γ = 180° − 40° − 75° = 65° (angle sum). Sine rule: b = a·sin β/sin α = 12·sin 75°/sin 40° = 12 × 0.9659/0.6428 ≈ 18.03.

**Answer:** b ≈ 18.03; γ = 65°

## Q8 — cosine rule, three sides

Triangle with sides 5, 7 and 8. Compute the largest angle (1 decimal°).

**Solution.** The largest angle γ faces the longest side 8: cos γ = (5² + 7² − 8²)/(2·5·7) = (25 + 49 − 64)/70 = 10/70 = 1/7, so γ = cos⁻¹(1/7) ≈ 81.8°.

**Answer:** ≈ 81.8° (exactly cos γ = 1/7)

## Q9 — equation with a double angle

Solve exactly on 0 ≤ x ≤ 2π: sin(2x) = sin x.

**Solution.** 2 sin x cos x = sin x ⇔ sin x (2 cos x − 1) = 0 ⇔ sin x = 0 or cos x = ½. sin x = 0: x = 0, π, 2π. cos x = ½: x = π/3, 5π/3.

**Answer:** x = 0, π/3, π, 5π/3, 2π

## Q10 — context: ladder against a wall

A 6.0 m ladder stands with its foot 1.5 m from a vertical wall on horizontal ground.

a. Compute the angle between the ladder and the ground (1 decimal°).

b. Compute how high the top of the ladder reaches (2 decimals).

**Solution.** a. cos θ = 1.5/6 = 0.25 ⇔ θ = cos⁻¹(0.25) ≈ 75.5°. b. Height = 6·sin θ = 6·√(1 − 0.25²) = 6·√15/4 = (3√15)/2 ≈ 5.81 m.

**Answer:** a. ≈ 75.5°; b. (3√15)/2 ≈ 5.81 m
