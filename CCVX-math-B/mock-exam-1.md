# Mock exam 1 — CCVX Mathematics B (practice)

Original practice exam written for this prep. Nothing is copied from CCVX or CvTE exams.

- Time: 180 minutes. Total: 76 points over 4 problems.
- Grade: 1 + 9 × (points scored)/76, rounded to one decimal.
- Work algebraically and exactly. Give exact answers (forms with √, π, e, ln) unless the question asks for a rounding.
- No graphing calculator. The formula sheet printed below is the one from the real CCVX wiskunde B exam. A protractor/ruler and a standard scientific calculator are allowed.
- Marking scheme: [mock-exam-1-marking-scheme.md](mock-exam-1-marking-scheme.md).

## Formula sheet

sin²(x) + cos²(x) = 1

sin(t + u) = sin t cos u + cos t sin u
sin(t − u) = sin t cos u − cos t sin u
cos(t + u) = cos t cos u − sin t sin u
cos(t − u) = cos t cos u + sin t sin u

sin(2t) = 2 sin t cos t
cos(2t) = cos²t − sin²t = 2 cos²t − 1 = 1 − 2 sin²t

## Problem 1 — The shifted exponential (20 points)

The function f is given by f(x) = x·eˣ − 2eˣ, with domain **R**.

a. (3; mathb-mock1-q01) Show that f′(x) = eˣ(x − 1).

b. (4; mathb-mock1-q02) Compute the extreme value of f exactly, and state whether it is a minimum or a maximum.

c. (4; mathb-mock1-q03) The line l touches the graph of f at the point where x = 2. Give an exact equation of l in the form y = ax + b.

d. (5; mathb-mock1-q04) Compute exactly the area of the region enclosed by the graph of f, the x-axis and the line x = 3.

e. (4; mathb-mock1-q05) Compute the intersection of the graph of f with the x-axis, and use it with part b and the end behaviour to explain why the horizontal line y = k, with −e < k < 0, meets the graph of f in exactly two points.

## Problem 2 — The water tank (18 points)

A cylindrical water tank loses water through a tap. The water height h (in cm) during the first 12 minutes after opening it behaves like a root curve:

h(t) = 32 − 2√(t + 4)  for 0 ≤ t ≤ 12 (t in minutes).

a. (3; mathb-mock1-q06) Compute exactly the water height at t = 0 and at t = 12.

b. (4; mathb-mock1-q07) Compute exactly the average change of the height per minute on the interval 0 ≤ t ≤ 5, and the instantaneous rate of change dh/dt at t = 5 (exact, in cm/min).

c. (5; mathb-mock1-q08) Compute exactly at which time the height equals 26 cm. Show all algebraic steps.

From t = 12 the tank is refilled; the height then follows h₂(t) = 24 + 10 sin(π/6 · (t − 12)) for 12 ≤ t ≤ 24.

d. (2; mathb-mock1-q09) Give the equilibrium level, amplitude and period of this sinusoid. Check the continuity of the model at t = 12.

e. (4; mathb-mock1-q10) Compute all times in 12 ≤ t ≤ 24 at which the height equals 29 cm exactly.

## Problem 3 — The circle and the point (20 points)

Circle c is given by (x − 2)² + (y + 1)² = 25, and point P(7, 4) lies outside c.

a. (3; mathb-mock1-q11) Give the centre M and the radius r of c, and compute |MP| in simplified root form.

b. (5; mathb-mock1-q12) Two tangents can be drawn from P to c. Compute their common tangent length |PT| (the distance from P to a tangent point T) exactly.

c. (6; mathb-mock1-q13) One tangent through P is the vertical line x = 7. Compute its tangent point T₁ on c, and show that the radius to T₁ is indeed perpendicular to this tangent.

d. (6; mathb-mock1-q14) Compute the exact coordinates of the second tangent point T₂, and give an equation of the second tangent. (Hint: T₂ = (x, y) satisfies (T₂ − M)·(T₂ − P) = 0 — the radius is perpendicular to the tangent.)

## Problem 4 — The patrol drone (18 points)

The shadow of a drone moves over a flat roof with motion equations

x(t) = 3 sin t,   y(t) = 2 sin(2t),   t ≥ 0 (t in seconds, distances in metres).

a. (2; mathb-mock1-q15) Give the amplitude and the period of the x-motion and of the y-motion.

b. (3; mathb-mock1-q16) Show with the least common multiple that the period of the whole motion is 2π seconds.

c. (5; mathb-mock1-q17) Compute the exact coordinates of all points of the path with a horizontal tangent for 0 < t < 2π.

d. (4; mathb-mock1-q18) Compute the exact path speed at t = π/2, and the velocity vector at that instant.

e. (4; mathb-mock1-q19) The path passes the origin at t = π. Compute the exact slope of the tangent to the path at that passage.
