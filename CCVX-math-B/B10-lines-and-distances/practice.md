### mathb-b10-q01 | points 2 | outcome o1 | difficulty easy

#### Question

A line passes through (5, 0) and (0, −3). Give its equation in the form y = ax + b.

#### Answer

y = (3/5)x − 3

#### Solution

Intercept form: x/5 + y/(−3) = 1 ⇔ 3x − 5y = 15 ⇔ y = (3/5)x − 3. Check: (5, 0) gives 3 − 3 = 0 ✓; (0, −3) ✓.

### mathb-b10-q02 | points 2 | outcome o1 | difficulty easy

#### Question

Compute the intersection of l: 2x + 3y = 12 and m: x − y = 1.

#### Answer

(3, 2)

#### Solution

From m: y = x − 1. Substitute into l: 2x + 3(x − 1) = 12 ⇔ 5x = 15 ⇔ x = 3, y = 2. Check: 6 + 6 = 12 ✓.

### mathb-b10-q03 | points 3 | outcome o2 | difficulty medium

#### Question

For each pair, state whether the lines are parallel, perpendicular or neither:

- l₁: y = ⅔x + 1 and l₂: 2x − 3y = 5
- l₁ and l₃: 6x + 4y = 1

#### Answer

l₁ ∥ l₂ (parallel); l₁ ⟂ l₃ (perpendicular)

#### Solution

l₂: 3y = 2x − 5 → slope ⅔ = slope of l₁, and the lines differ (at x = 0: l₂ gives y = −5/3 ≠ 1), so parallel. l₃: 4y = −6x + 1 → slope −3/2; product with l₁'s slope: (2/3)(−3/2) = −1, so perpendicular.

### mathb-b10-q04 | points 2 | outcome o4 | difficulty easy

#### Question

Compute |AB| for A(−2, 1) and B(4, 8), exactly and to 2 decimals.

#### Answer

√85 ≈ 9.22

#### Solution

Δx = 6, Δy = 7: |AB| = √(36 + 49) = √85 ≈ 9.22.

### mathb-b10-q05 | points 4 | outcome o5 | difficulty medium

#### Question

Find the foot Q of the perpendicular from P(3, 4) to l: y = x − 1 (the projection of P on l), and the distance from P to l.

#### Answer

Q(4, 3); d = √2 ≈ 1.41

#### Solution

Perpendicular slope: −1 (product with 1 must be −1); through P: y = −x + 7 (check: 4 = −3 + 7 ✓). Intersect: x − 1 = −x + 7 ⇔ x = 4, y = 3: Q(4, 3). Distance: √((4−3)² + (3−4)²) = √2 ≈ 1.41.

### mathb-b10-q06 | points 3 | outcome o2 | difficulty medium

#### Question

Given l: y = √3·x + 2 and m: y = (√3/3)x − 1.

a. Give both direction angles.

b. Compute the angle between l and m.

#### Answer

a. 60° and 30°; b. 30°

#### Solution

a. tan α = √3 → α = 60°; tan β = √3/3 → β = 30°. b. Difference: 60° − 30° = 30°. Since 30° < 90°, this is the angle between the lines.

### mathb-b10-q07 | points 4 | outcome o6 | difficulty medium

#### Question

Give the equation of the perpendicular bisector of A(0, 0) and B(6, 2).

#### Answer

y = −3x + 10

#### Solution

Midpoint: ((0+6)/2, (0+2)/2) = (3, 1). Slope AB = (2 − 0)/(6 − 0) = 1/3, so the bisector slope is −3: y = −3(x − 3) + 1 = −3x + 10. Check a general point P(x, y) with |PA| = |PB|: x² + y² = (x − 6)² + (y − 2)² ⇔ 0 = −12x + 36 − 4y + 4 ⇔ 12x + 4y = 40 ⇔ 3x + y = 10 ⇔ y = −3x + 10 ✓.

### mathb-b10-q08 | points 5 | outcome o7 | difficulty hard

#### Question

Compute the equations of both angle bisectors of l: x + y = 2 and m: x − y = 2.

#### Answer

y = 0 and x = 2

#### Solution

P(x, y) is a bisector point when d(P, l) = d(P, m): |x + y − 2|/√(1² + 1²) = |x − y − 2|/√(1² + 1²) ⇔ x + y − 2 = ±(x − y − 2). Plus sign: 2y = 0 → y = 0. Minus sign: 2x − 4 = 0 → x = 2. Check with direction vectors: l has direction (1, −1), m has (1, 1); they meet at (2, 0) (solving the system). The bisectors are the symmetry axes through the intersection: the horizontal line y = 0 and the vertical line x = 2 ✓.
