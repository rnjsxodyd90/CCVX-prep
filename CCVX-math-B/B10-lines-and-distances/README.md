# B10 — Lines and distances (coordinate geometry)

- **Dutch:** *meetkunde met coördinaten: lijnen en afstanden*
- **CCVX syllabus:** programme point 14 and the section *meetkunde met coördinaten* of the concept list.

## Learning outcomes (CCVX syllabus)

In a right-angled coordinate system with equal units on both axes, the candidate can:

1. Work with straight lines (*rechte lijnen*): the general form mx + ny = c; the common form y = ax + b (not for vertical lines); the slope (*richtingscoëfficiënt*) Δy/Δx = (y_B − y_A)/(x_B − x_A); and the intercept form x/p + y/q = 1 for the line through (p, 0) and (0, q) with p, q ≠ 0.
2. Use angles: the direction angle (*richtingshoek*) of a line satisfies tan(α) = slope with −90° < α < 90°; the angle between two lines is the smallest angle at their intersection, so 0° ≤ β ≤ 90°; two lines are perpendicular exactly when the product of their slopes is −1, and conversely.
3. Set up perpendicular lines (*loodlijnen*): the perpendicular to y = ax + b has slope −1/a.
4. Compute the distance between points A(a₁, a₂) and B(b₁, b₂): |AB| = √((b₁ − a₁)² + (b₂ − a₂)²), also written d(A, B).
5. Compute the projection of a point on a line, the distance between a point and a line, and the distance between two parallel lines, using perpendicular lines; the distance formula may be used but is not assumed known.
6. Use the perpendicular bisector (*middelloodlijn*) of points A and B: the set of all points P with |PA| = |PB|.
7. Use the angle bisector pair (*bissectricepaar*) of lines l and m: the set of all points P with d(P, l) = d(P, m).
8. Set up algebraic representations of figures in a given or self-chosen coordinate system and use them to solve geometric problems.
