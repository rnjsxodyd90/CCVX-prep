# A8 — Sequences

- **Dutch:** *rijen*
- **CCVX syllabus:** programme point 8 and the section *rijen* of the concept list.

## Learning outcomes (CCVX syllabus)

The candidate can recognise and describe the behaviour of a sequence (*rij*) and perform calculations on it, in particular for arithmetic and geometric sequences. In detail:

1. Work with the two formula types: the direct formula (*directe formule*), which computes a term from its index (u_n in terms of n), and the recursive formula (*recursieve formule*), which computes a term from the previous one (u_{n+1} in terms of u_n, with u_0 given); convert between them for the standard types.
2. Recognise and use the arithmetic sequence (*rekenkundige rij*): constant difference; recursive u_{n+1} = u_n + a, direct u_n = u_0 + a·n (also u_n = u_1 + a·(n − 1)).
3. Recognise and use the geometric sequence (*meetkundige rij*): constant ratio; recursive u_{n+1} = r·u_n, direct u_n = rⁿ·u_0 (also u_n = rⁿ⁻¹·u_1); the explicit exponential character.
4. Use the sum formulas (formula sheet): for the arithmetic sequence, Sum = ½ · (number of terms) · (first term + last term) = ½·count·(u_e + u_l); for the geometric sequence, Sum = (u_{l+1} − u_e)/(r − 1) with r ≠ 1 (e = index of the first term, l = index of the last term).
5. Compute the index of a term with a given value: set the direct formula equal to the term and solve (for the geometric sequence with logarithms).
6. Apply sequences to contexts: savings and interest, instalments/annuities (basic forms), standing and movement patterns, population models; connect geometric sequences to exponential growth ([A04](../A04-exponential-growth/)).
7. Describe the behaviour of a sequence from its formula: constant, increasing, decreasing, alternation, convergence to a limit (e.g. u_{n+1} = ½·u_n + 10 with suitable u_0 converges; find the limit L from L = ½L + 10).
