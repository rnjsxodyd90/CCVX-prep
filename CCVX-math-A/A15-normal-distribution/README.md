# A15 — The normal distribution

- **Dutch:** *de normale verdeling*
- **CCVX syllabus:** programme point 14 and the section *de normale verdeling* of the concept list.

## Learning outcomes (CCVX syllabus)

The candidate can:

1. Handle the normal distribution (*normale verdeling*) and the Gaussian curve (*Gausskromme*) as a model and approximation of a frequency distribution, with the parameters μ (mean, = expectation) and σ (standard deviation): mean = peak and axis of symmetry, σ = the distance from μ to the inflection points.
2. Use the symmetry: 50% of the observations lies below the mean; and the rules of thumb (*vuistregels*): 68% of the observations lies between μ − σ and μ + σ, and 95% between μ − 2σ and μ + 2σ. Consequences to know cold: 16% beyond μ + σ, 2.5% beyond μ + 2σ, 0.15% beyond μ + 3σ (68–95–99.7).
3. Compute probabilities and percentages for normal situations with the rules of thumb only (the exam gives you values matching these boundaries).
4. Compute μ and/or σ when percentages at given boundaries are known — e.g. "84% of a population weighs more than 80 kg and 2.5% more than 110 kg" translates to μ − σ = 80 and μ + 2σ = 110 (solve the system).
5. Approximate discrete distributions with the normal distribution: for a binomial X with np ≥ 5 use a normal Y with μ_Y = np and σ_Y = √(np(1−p)).
6. Apply the continuity correction (*continuïteitscorrectie*) when approximating discrete counts: P(X ≤ k) = P(X < k + ½) (fill up to the middle of the "bin").
