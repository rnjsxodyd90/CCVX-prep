### matha-a02-q01 | points 2 | outcome o1 | difficulty easy

#### Question

A line has slope −3 and passes through (0, 5). Give its equation in the form y = ax + b and its x-intercept.

#### Answer

y = −3x + 5; x-intercept (5/3, 0)

#### Solution

The intercept (0, 5) gives b = 5 directly: y = −3x + 5. Zero: 0 = −3x + 5 ⇔ x = 5/3, so (5/3, 0).

### matha-a02-q02 | points 3 | outcome o1 | difficulty easy

#### Question

Write 4x − 2y = 10 in the form y = ax + b, and give the slope, the y-intercept and the x-intercept.

#### Answer

y = 2x − 5; slope 2; y-intercept (0, −5); x-intercept (5/2, 0)

#### Solution

−2y = −4x + 10 ⇔ y = 2x − 5. So slope 2, through (0, −5); zero at 2x = 5 ⇔ x = 5/2.

### matha-a02-q03 | points 3 | outcome o3 | difficulty medium

#### Question

Give the equation of the line through A(−1, 4) that is parallel to the line y = ½x + 9.

#### Answer

y = ½x + 9/2

#### Solution

Parallel means the same slope ½. Through A: 4 = ½·(−1) + b ⇔ b = 4.5 = 9/2. Line: y = ½x + 9/2.

### matha-a02-q04 | points 3 | outcome o4 | difficulty medium

#### Question

Compute the intersection of the lines 2x + 3y = 12 and x − 2y = −1.

#### Answer

(3, 2)

#### Solution

From the second: x = 2y − 1. Substitute: 2(2y − 1) + 3y = 12 ⇔ 7y − 2 = 12 ⇔ y = 2, then x = 3. Check the first: 6 + 6 = 12 ✓; the second: 3 − 4 = −1 ✓.

### matha-a02-q05 | points 4 | outcome o5 | difficulty medium

#### Question

A print run of 100 posters costs €450, and 300 posters cost €850. Assume a linear cost formula C(x) = fixed + per-piece.

a. Give the formula.

b. Compute the cost of 180 posters.

#### Answer

a. C(x) = 250 + 2x; b. €610

#### Solution

a. Slope: (850 − 450)/(300 − 100) = 400/200 = €2 per poster. Fixed: 450 − 2·100 = €250. So C(x) = 250 + 2x. Check at 300: 250 + 600 = 850 ✓. b. C(180) = 250 + 360 = €610 (interpolation).

### matha-a02-q06 | points 3 | outcome o6 | difficulty easy

#### Question

Two quantities x and y are inversely proportional with x·y = 36.

a. Give y when x = 9.

b. The value of y doubles compared with part a. What happens to x?

#### Answer

a. y = 4; b. x halves from 9 to 4.5

#### Solution

a. y = 36/9 = 4. b. y = 8 gives x = 36/8 = 4.5: doubling y halves x — the defining behaviour of inverse proportionality.

### matha-a02-q07 | points 2 | outcome o8 | difficulty easy

#### Question

Solve: 3x − 2 < x + 8.

#### Answer

x < 5

#### Solution

3x − 2 < x + 8 ⇔ 2x < 10 ⇔ x < 5. (Reading: the line y = 3x − 2 stays below y = x + 8 until their intersection at x = 5.)

### matha-a02-q08 | points 4 | outcome o7 | difficulty medium

#### Question

City taxi tariff A: €4.00 start plus €1.80 per km. Tariff B: €2.50 start plus €2.10 per km.

a. For which distance are the two fares equal?

b. Which tariff is cheaper for a 12 km ride, and by how much?

#### Answer

a. 5 km; b. A, by €2.10

#### Solution

a. 4 + 1.80x = 2.50 + 2.10x ⇔ 1.50 = 0.30x ⇔ x = 5 km (both then €13). b. A: 4 + 21.60 = €25.60; B: 2.50 + 25.20 = €27.70. So **A** is cheaper by 27.70 − 25.60 = €2.10. (A costs more to start but less per km, so A wins beyond the break-even point of 5 km.)

### matha-a02-q09 | points 4 | outcome o7 | difficulty medium

#### Question

For a product, demand is q_d = 200 − 5p and supply is q_s = 20p − 50 (q in thousands, p in euros). Compute the equilibrium price and quantity.

#### Answer

p = 10, q = 150 thousand

#### Solution

200 − 5p = 20p − 50 ⇔ 250 = 25p ⇔ p = 10. Then q = 200 − 50 = 150 (thousand). Check supply: 200 − 50 = 150 ✓.

### matha-a02-q10 | points 4 | outcome o3 | difficulty medium

#### Question

a. Give the equation of the line through (1, 9) and (4, 0).

b. Where does it meet the line y = x?

#### Answer

a. y = −3x + 12; b. (3, 3)

#### Solution

a. Slope: (0 − 9)/(4 − 1) = −3. Through (1, 9): 9 = −3 + b ⇔ b = 12: y = −3x + 12. Check (4, 0): −12 + 12 = 0 ✓. b. −3x + 12 = x ⇔ 4x = 12 ⇔ x = 3; point (3, 3).
