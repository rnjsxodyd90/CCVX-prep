# A2 — Linear relations

- **Dutch:** *rechte lijnen en lineaire verbanden*
- **CCVX syllabus:** programme points 4 and 5, and the section *rechte lijnen en lineaire verbanden* of the concept list.

## Learning outcomes (CCVX syllabus)

The candidate can recognise and use the characteristics of linear functions in graph, table and formula. In detail:

1. Work with the equation of a straight line: the general form mx + ny = c, with n = 0 giving a vertical line and m = 0 a horizontal one; and for non-vertical lines the form y = ax + b with slope (*richtingscoëfficiënt*) a and y-intercept b.
2. Compute the slope of the line through A(x_A, y_A) and B(x_B, y_B): a = Δy/Δx = (y_B − y_A)/(x_B − x_A).
3. Set up the equation of a line from the slope and a point (substitute into y = ax + b), or from two points.
4. Compute the intersection of two lines by eliminating y (solving the system).
5. Interpolate and extrapolate linearly between/behind known values.
6. Use direct proportionality (*recht evenredig*, y = ax, through the origin) and inverse proportionality (*omgekeerd evenredig*, y = a/x).
7. Set up linear formulas from contexts (cost = fixed + per-unit, travel = distance/speed), combine formulas correctly with brackets, and interpret slopes and intercepts in context (including negative slopes and non-meaningful regions in applications).
8. Solve the corresponding equations and inequalities algebraically (no graphing calculator!).
