# CCVX Mathematics A

Study material for the [CCVX](https://www.ccvx.nl/index%20-%20EN.html) entrance exam (*voortentamen*) in Mathematics A, organised by the official CCVX syllabus: [Leerstof CCVW wiskunde A 2018](syllabus/leerstof-ccvw-wiskunde-a-2018.pdf) (in force since December 2018, unchanged for 2026/2027). An [English text version](syllabus/leerstof-wiskunde-a.en.md) of the syllabus is included for reference.

## The exam

- Written exam with open questions, 3 hours, in Dutch or English, in Utrecht only. Registration closes 10 days before the exam; the fee is €98 per attempt.
- Dates in 2026/27: Monday 14 December 2026 (13:30–16:30), around 16 April 2027 and around 22 July 2027.
- No graphing calculator, and no calculator that computes integrals. A standard scientific calculator (Casio fx-82 or TI-30 class) is allowed.
- All computations must be algebraic. The domain Statistics and Probability **is** examined (in the Dutch VWO it is only in the school exam).
- A formula sheet (quadratic formula, differentiation rules, logarithm rules, sum formulas for sequences, probability rules, binomial and normal distribution, critical values for testing) is printed on the last two pages of the exam.
- Bring: valid ID, a pen (pencil only for graphs), a ruler or set square, a watch (no smartwatch), food and drink.

## Layout

There is one folder per syllabus subdomain. Each folder has:

- a `README.md` with the learning outcomes in English, with the Dutch terms of the syllabus in brackets;
- a `notes.md` with English explanations of every outcome, with worked examples at the end;
- a `practice.md` with original practice questions and full worked solutions. Every question is original material written for this prep: nothing is copied from CCVX or CvTE exams.
