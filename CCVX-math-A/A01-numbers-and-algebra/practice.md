### matha-a01-q01 | points 2 | outcome o1 | difficulty easy

#### Question

A webshop raises a price by 15% and later lowers the new price by 10%. The original price was €80. Compute the final price, and the net percentage change.

#### Answer

€82.80; +3.5%

#### Solution

80 × 1.15 = 92; 92 × 0.90 = 82.80. Net factor 1.15 × 0.90 = 1.035, so +3.5% — not +5% (percentages multiply, they do not add).

### matha-a01-q02 | points 2 | outcome o1 | difficulty easy

#### Question

Write in scientific notation: (a) 0.00000045; (b) 3.2 million.

#### Answer

(a) 4.5 × 10⁻⁷; (b) 3.2 × 10⁶

#### Solution

Move the decimal point until the coefficient lies in [1, 10): (a) 0.00000045 = 4.5 × 10⁻⁷ (seven places left); (b) 3 200 000 = 3.2 × 10⁶.

### matha-a01-q03 | points 3 | outcome o2 | difficulty easy

#### Question

Compute exactly, as simplified fractions: (a) 5/6 − 3/4; (b) (2/3)/(4/9).

#### Answer

(a) 1/12; (b) 3/2

#### Solution

(a) Common denominator 12: 10/12 − 9/12 = 1/12. (b) Dividing by 4/9 is multiplying by 9/4: (2/3)×(9/4) = 18/12 = 3/2.

### matha-a01-q04 | points 3 | outcome o4 | difficulty medium

#### Question

Convert: (a) 90 km/h to m/s; (b) an infusion rate of 0.75 L per 20 seconds to L/min.

#### Answer

(a) 25 m/s; (b) 2.25 L/min

#### Solution

(a) 90 km = 90 000 m, 1 h = 3600 s: 90 000/3600 = 25 m/s. (b) Per second: 0.75/20 = 0.0375 L/s; per minute: 0.0375 × 60 = 2.25 L/min.

### matha-a01-q05 | points 3 | outcome o3 | difficulty medium

#### Question

The area of a trapezium is A = ½·(b + B)·h. Express B in terms of A, b and h.

#### Answer

B = 2A/h − b

#### Solution

2A = (b + B)·h ⇔ 2A/h = b + B ⇔ B = 2A/h − b.

### matha-a01-q06 | points 3 | outcome o8 | difficulty medium

#### Question

Solve: 3/(x − 2) = 2/(x + 1).

#### Answer

x = −7

#### Solution

Cross-multiply (x ≠ 2, −1): 3(x + 1) = 2(x − 2) ⇔ 3x + 3 = 2x − 4 ⇔ x = −7. Check: 3/(−9) = −1/3 and 2/(−6) = −1/3 ✓.

### matha-a01-q07 | points 4 | outcome o6 | difficulty medium

#### Question

a. Solve exactly: x² − 6x + 8 = 0.

b. Solve the inequality x² − 6x + 8 > 0.

#### Answer

a. x = 2 ∨ x = 4; b. x < 2 ∨ x > 4

#### Solution

a. Factoring: (x − 2)(x − 4) = 0 ⇔ x = 2 or x = 4. (abc check: D = 36 − 32 = 4, x = (6 ± 2)/2 ✓.) b. The parabola opens upwards and lies above zero outside the roots: x < 2 or x > 4.

### matha-a01-q08 | points 3 | outcome o6 | difficulty easy

#### Question

Solve: √(2x − 3) = 3.

#### Answer

x = 6

#### Solution

Square (both sides ≥ 0 ✓): 2x − 3 = 9 ⇔ x = 6. Check: √9 = 3 ✓.

### matha-a01-q09 | points 3 | outcome o8 | difficulty easy

#### Question

Solve the system: 2a + 5b = 21 and a − b = 7.

#### Answer

a = 8, b = 1

#### Solution

From the second equation: a = b + 7. Substitute: 2(b + 7) + 5b = 21 ⇔ 7b + 14 = 21 ⇔ b = 1, then a = 8. Check: 16 + 5 = 21 ✓; 8 − 1 = 7 ✓.

### matha-a01-q10 | points 4 | outcome o6 | difficulty hard

#### Question

Solve: x³ = 4x. (Hint: do not divide by x.)

#### Answer

x = −2, 0 or 2

#### Solution

Bring all to one side and factor — dividing by x would lose the solution x = 0: x³ − 4x = 0 ⇔ x(x² − 4) = 0 ⇔ x(x − 2)(x + 2) = 0 ⇔ x = 0, x = 2 or x = −2. All three check in the original equation.
