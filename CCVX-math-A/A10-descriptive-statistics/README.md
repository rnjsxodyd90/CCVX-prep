# A10 — Descriptive statistics

- **Dutch:** *beschrijvende statistiek*
- **CCVX syllabus:** programme point 10 and the section *beschrijvende statistiek* of the concept list (the syllabus notes this topic appears *only to a very limited extent* in the exam).

## Learning outcomes (CCVX syllabus)

The candidate can:

1. Determine the mode (*modus*: the most frequent value) and the median (*mediaan*: the middle value after sorting; for an even count, the mean of the two middle values) of a series of individual observations (*losse waarnemingsgetallen*).
2. Compute the mean (*gemiddelde*) and the standard deviation (*standaardafwijking*) of a population of limited size, using the statistics function of a simple (non-graphing) calculator; know the defining formulas: mean = (Σ values)/n, and the standard deviation as the root of the mean squared deviation (SD, dividing by n — the population version).
3. Compute the mean and the standard deviation for a frequency distribution (*frequentieverdeling*): use the class midpoint with the frequency as weight, or exact values with relative frequencies.
4. Interpret the measures: effect of an extreme value on median vs mean; why the standard deviation measures spread; choose a suitable measure in a simple case.
