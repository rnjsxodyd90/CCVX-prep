# A7 — Applications of the derivative

- **Dutch:** *toepassingen van de afgeleide*
- **CCVX syllabus:** programme point 7 and the section *toepassingen van de afgeleide* of the concept list.

## Learning outcomes (CCVX syllabus)

The candidate can:

1. Use the derivative as slope: f′(a) is the slope of the tangent line (*raaklijn*) to the graph at the point (a, f(a)).
2. Describe the increasing/decreasing behaviour: the graph rises where f′(x) > 0 and falls where f′(x) < 0; describe the changing behaviour (*veranderingsgedrag*) of a function from the derivative (how fast it grows or falls where).
3. Compute the positions of the minima and maxima (*extremen*) by solving f′(x) = 0 algebraically, and determine the nature of the extrema with a sketch of the graph; the corresponding points are the vertices (*toppen*) of the graph.
4. Compute extreme values in contexts (maximal profit, minimal cost, optimal dose, optimal number) with correct interpretation back into the context, including checking boundary values when the domain is an interval.
5. Work with minimal and maximal growth rate (*minimale en maximale groeisnelheid*): the derivative is itself a function (the growth speed); its extremes can be found by differentiating again — the candidate can compute the extreme growth rates in suitable simple cases.
6. Combine the derivative with the model types of this syllabus: quadratics and cubics, power functions, exponential and logarithmic models (saturation), and the sinusoids of [A09](../A09-sine-functions/).
