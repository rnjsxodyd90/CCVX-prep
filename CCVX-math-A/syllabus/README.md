# Syllabus documents

Source: [CCVX](https://www.ccvx.nl/), Mathematics section.

- [leerstof-ccvw-wiskunde-a-2018.pdf](leerstof-ccvw-wiskunde-a-2018.pdf): the official CCVX Mathematics A syllabus (*Leerstof CCVW wiskunde A*, in force since December 2018). Direct link: <https://www.ccvx.nl/Leerstof%20CCVW%20wiskunde%20A%202018.pdf>
- [leerstof-wiskunde-a.nl.txt](leerstof-wiskunde-a.nl.txt): the Dutch text extracted from the PDF.
- [leerstof-wiskunde-a.en.md](leerstof-wiskunde-a.en.md): an English translation of the exam programme and the concept list, made for this prep.

The syllabus says the exam programme is based on the Dutch VWO exam programme wiskunde A for 2019, with two important differences: no graphing calculator or other ICT is allowed, and the domain Statistics and Probability is examined (in the VWO it is only in the school exam). Recommended study books named in the syllabus: Getal en Ruimte vwo A (11th or 12th edition, parts 1AC, 2AC, 3A, 4A) or equivalent; Wiswijs for the foundational and algebraic skills.

The free official CCVX example exams for mathematics are linked from <https://www.ccvx.nl/> under *Voorbeeldexamens*. They are not copied into this folder — see the hard copyright rules of this repository.
