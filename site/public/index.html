Questions

Is this official CCVX material?

No. This is independent study material. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by the CCVX or any university. The official sample exams are free on ccvx.nl, and we link to them instead of copying them.

What do I get for EUR 79?

One subject, fully: all notes, all original practice questions with worked solutions, and the mock exams with marking schemes. Twelve months of access, VAT included. Bundles cost less per subject: any 2 for EUR 139, any 3 for EUR 189, all 5 for EUR 279.

Which subjects are ready?

Chemistry is being finished for the exam of 26 November 2026. Biology, Mathematics A and Mathematics B are in preparation; each subject page says what is inside today.

Is everything in English?

Yes. The notes, questions and mock exams are in English and follow the English CCVX syllabus and BINAS conventions.

Can I get a refund?

Access starts immediately after payment. At checkout you confirm that you waive the 14-day withdrawal right, as the law allows for digital content that starts right away. The refund policy explains the details.

Does EUR 79 include the exam fee?

No. You register and pay the CCVX separately; the exam costs EUR 98 per sitting.

What may I bring to the exam?

The CCVX allows a BINAS book without notes in it. Our notes use BINAS table numbers, so the book feels familiar on exam day.

Can I study on my phone?

Yes. The reader works on desktop and mobile. A printed BINAS and paper for working out are still worth having.
