Privacy

Short version: we keep your email address and your purchases so you can sign in and read your subjects. We do not run advertisements and we do not sell data.

What we store

  • Your email address and sign-in method, in Firebase Authentication (Google). Purpose: your account.
  • Your purchases: which subjects you bought, and until when, in Firestore (Google). Purpose: giving you access.
  • Your Stripe customer id, linked to your account, in Firestore. Purpose: connecting payments to access.
  • Payment details themselves (card numbers and the like) never reach us; they live at Stripe only.

What we do not store

We do not store your practice answers, typed work in the reader, or usage profiles for advertising. Timed mock runs stay in your browser.

Processors

Google (Firebase) hosts the site, accounts and purchase records. Stripe (Stripe Payments Europe) processes payments and handles VAT invoices. Neither may use your data for their own purposes beyond running their service.

Retention

Account and purchase records stay while you have an account and as long as tax law requires (7 years for sales records in the Netherlands). You may ask for a copy or deletion at any time by writing to us at our address.

Cookies

Only functional storage for sign-in and preferences. No tracking cookies, no analytics cookies, so no cookie banner.

Your rights

You have the right to see, correct, export or delete your data, and to complain to the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens.
