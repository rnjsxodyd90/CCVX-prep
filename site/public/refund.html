Refund and withdrawal policy

Your library and purchases

The 14-day rule, honestly explained

EU consumer law normally gives you 14 days to withdraw from an online purchase. For digital content that you use right away, the law lets the seller ask you to waive that right before delivery starts. That is what the checkout checkbox does:

"I understand that access starts immediately after payment, and I waive my 14-day withdrawal right, as the law allows for digital content that starts right away."

Once you confirm that checkbox and pay, you cannot cancel simply because you changed your mind, because delivery has fully started.

What stays protected

  • Before you confirm the waiver: you have the full 14-day withdrawal right, and you keep it if you do not buy.
  • If the content is faulty: you are entitled to a repair, a workable replacement, or a proportional refund, and to a full refund when repair or replacement is impossible. Faulty means, for example, unreadable pages, missing marking schemes for content that should have them, or access that does not work.
  • If we misdescribe the product, normal consumer law applies in full.
