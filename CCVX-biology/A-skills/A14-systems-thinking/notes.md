## What the skill means

- Distinguishing levels of organisation (*organisatieniveaus*), working out relationships within and between them, and explaining how biological units (*biologische eenheden*) at each level maintain themselves and develop. *(CvTE A14)*
- The levels are molecule, cell, organ, organism, population and ecosystem, with the Earth system (*Systeem Aarde*) above them. *(CvTE §1.1; CvTE appendix 2)*
- The five system concepts that name CCVX domains B to F are self-regulation (*zelfregulatie*), self-organisation (*zelforganisatie*), interaction (*interactie*), reproduction (*reproductie*) and evolution (*evolutie*). *(CvTE §1.1)*

## Parts and relationships within one level

- Within one level, the parts of a system and the relationships between them (structure and working) together determine its function and keep it going. *(CvTE A14 spec 1)*
- The CvTE's example is the heart and blood vessels: valves keep the blood flowing one way, thick elastic artery walls withstand and smooth out the pressure, and thin capillary walls allow exchange with the tissues ([B03](../../B-self-regulation/B03-metabolism-in-organisms/README.md)).
- Self-regulation by negative feedback: a rise in blood glucose makes the pancreas release insulin, liver and muscle cells take up glucose, and the level falls back ([B04](../../B-self-regulation/B04-self-regulation-of-organisms/README.md)).

## Influence from lower and higher levels

- How a system works at one level is influenced by the levels below and above it. *(CvTE A14 spec 2)*
- The CvTE's example is energy flow: it starts with photosynthesis in chloroplasts and continues through consumers to the whole ecosystem ([B08](../../B-self-regulation/B08-regulation-of-ecosystems/README.md)).
- From below: one mutation in the CFTR gene changes a channel protein in cell membranes, mucus in the lungs becomes thick, and the whole organism has cystic fibrosis. From above: hormones made elsewhere in the body switch genes on or off in target cells, as when a steroid hormone and its receptor act as a transcription factor ([C01](../../C-self-organization/C01-self-organization-of-cells/README.md), [D01](../../D-interaction/D01-molecular-interaction/README.md)).

## Emergent properties

- A system at a higher level can have properties that the levels below it don't have: emergent properties (*emergente eigenschappen*). Self-organisation produces such new higher-level units: "the whole is more than the sum of the parts". *(CvTE A14 spec 3; CvTE §1.1)*
- CCVX's example is the biological clock: single clock cells peak at different times of day, but together they can register day length and the time of year ([C-self-organization](../../C-self-organization/README.md)).
- A population has a density, a birth rate and allele frequencies, which no single organism has ([F02](../../F-evolution/F02-speciation/README.md)); an ecosystem has food webs and nutrient cycles, which no single population has ([C03](../../C-self-organization/C03-self-organization-of-ecosystems/README.md)).
- **Trap:** a total such as the biomass of an ecosystem is only the sum of its parts, not an emergent property; an emergent property comes from the interactions between the parts.
