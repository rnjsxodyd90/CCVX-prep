# A3 — Reflecting on Learning: notes

These are English explanations of every learning outcome in [README.md](README.md), and every topic is *drafted*: written from the CCVX syllabus, the Dutch CvTE syllabus and standard VWO biology, not yet checked against an open source text or exam question.

- **In Dutch VWO:** the CvTE §1.4 table marks A3 for the central and the school exam, but prints the A3 end term grey, so the Dutch central exam doesn't test it. CCVX keeps it and tests it inside content questions.
- **Dutch source:** [CvTE syllabus biologie vwo 2027](https://www.examenblad.nl/2027/vwo/documenten/syllabus-biologie-vwo-2027) (version 2, July 2025), domain A, A3 *Reflecteren op leren*. A1–A4 have only an attainment target (*eindterm*), which the CvTE doesn't specify further; a citation like *(CvTE A3)* means that attainment target.

Dutch terms are in brackets. Binas tables are named, not numbered, because the numbers differ between editions.

## What the skill means

- Reflecting on your own interests, motivation and learning process while acquiring subject knowledge and skills (*reflecteren op eigen belangstelling, motivatie en leerproces*). *(CvTE A3)*
- A written exam can't test personal reflection, and CCVX leaves out what can't be tested in writing *(CCVX syllabus §1)*. For CCVX this skill is a way to prepare, not exam content.

## Using it to prepare

- Check yourself against every outcome in each README: can you explain it without notes, with an example and the Dutch term?
- The CCVX syllabus points to its specimen exams and past papers on the CCVX website for examples of questions *(CCVX syllabus §1)*. Note which kinds of question cost you points: calculations, cause–effect explanations or reading graphs.
- **Trap:** recognising a term is not the same as being able to use it; test yourself by explaining it in your own words.
