## What the skill means

- Reasoning about what internal or external changes do to a community (*levensgemeenschap*) or an ecosystem, especially in sustainability (*duurzaamheid*) contexts. *(CvTE A12)*
- A good answer follows the chain of effects step by step, through the organisms and the abiotic factors involved.

## An ecosystem as a network

- An ecosystem is a complex whole of biotic and abiotic components and their interactions, which together keep it going and decide its development and species composition. *(CvTE A12 spec 1)*
- Changes come from inside, such as a growing population or succession, or from outside, such as a drought, an introduced species or human action ([C03](../../C-self-organization/C03-self-organization-of-ecosystems/README.md), [B08](../../B-self-regulation/B08-regulation-of-ecosystems/README.md)).
- Example: if a predator disappears, its prey becomes more numerous and eats more vegetation, which changes the plants and the other animals that depend on them.

## Cycles, energy flow and food webs

- Ecosystems are regulated by the carbon and nitrogen cycles, energy flows and food relationships over several trophic levels. *(CvTE A12 spec 2)*
- Energy flows one way and is lost as heat at every step: with about 10% passed on per level, 10 000 kJ in the producers gives about 1000 kJ in the herbivores and 100 kJ in the carnivores ([B08](../../B-self-regulation/B08-regulation-of-ecosystems/README.md)).
- Matter cycles: decomposers turn dead organic matter back into CO₂ and ammonium, nitrifying bacteria turn ammonium into nitrate, and producers take these up again ([B08](../../B-self-regulation/B08-regulation-of-ecosystems/README.md), [01-chemistry](../../0-basic-knowledge/01-chemistry/README.md)).
- Eutrophication: extra nitrate and phosphate cause an algal bloom, the algae die, decomposers use up the O₂ while breaking them down, and fish die ([B08](../../B-self-regulation/B08-regulation-of-ecosystems/README.md), [F03](../../F-evolution/F03-biodiversity/README.md)).

## Human actions with large effects

- Human actions at a lower level of organisation can cause large changes at higher levels. *(CvTE A12 spec 3)*
- DDT, a persistent, fat-soluble insecticide, accumulated along food chains; birds of prey at the top laid eggs with thin shells, and their populations crashed ([D05](../../D-interaction/D05-interaction-in-ecosystems/README.md)).
- Burning fossil fuels raises the CO₂ concentration in the air, which strengthens the greenhouse effect and changes climate and ecosystems worldwide ([B08](../../B-self-regulation/B08-regulation-of-ecosystems/README.md)).
- The B08 context, importing soya from Brazil as pig feed, connects land clearance for soya there with manure surpluses, and so extra nitrate and phosphate, in the Netherlands.
- **Trap:** skipping links in the chain: "fertiliser kills fish" leaves out the algae, the decomposers and the O₂.
