## What the skill means

- Reasoning from the form (*vorm*) of a biological object to its function (*functie*) and back, at any level of organisation. "Form" covers the spatial shape, the material, the construction and the process. *(CvTE A11)*

## From form to function

- Given a structure, explain what it makes possible. *(CvTE A11 spec 1)*
- Alveoli: millions of small, thin-walled sacs surrounded by capillaries give a large, thin exchange surface, so gases diffuse fast (Fick's law, [B03](../../B-self-regulation/B03-metabolism-in-organisms/README.md)).
- Red blood cells are biconcave and, in mammals, have no nucleus: the shape gives a large surface and short diffusion distances, and without a nucleus there is more room for haemoglobin.
- An enzyme's active site fits its substrate ([B02](../../B-self-regulation/B02-metabolism-in-cells/README.md)), and a myelinated axon conducts impulses faster than an unmyelinated one ([B04](../../B-self-regulation/B04-self-regulation-of-organisms/README.md)).

## From function to form

- Given a function, work out what form the parts need: what is the whole for, and what does that demand of its parts? *(CvTE A11 spec 2)*
- Gas exchange needs a large, thin, moist surface with a good blood supply; lungs and gills meet these demands in different ways ([B03](../../B-self-regulation/B03-metabolism-in-organisms/README.md)).
- Pumping blood through the whole body needs high pressure, so the left ventricle has a thicker muscle wall than the right one, which pumps blood only through the lungs.
- Carrying water up a tall plant needs strong, open tubes: xylem consists of dead cells with lignified walls. The dialysis machine in [A06](../A06-design/README.md) applies the same thinking to a design.

## Across levels of organisation

- The structure and working (*bouw en werking*) of a system explain its function, sometimes across several levels of organisation. *(CvTE A11 spec 3)*
- Actin and myosin filaments slide along each other, which shortens each sarcomere, then the muscle fibre, then the whole muscle ([B06](../../B-self-regulation/B06-movement-of-the-organism/README.md)).
- In sickle-cell anaemia one amino acid in haemoglobin changes (glutamic acid to valine), the molecules clump together at low O₂, the red blood cells become sickle-shaped and they block capillaries ([B01](../../B-self-regulation/B01-protein-synthesis/README.md), [F01](../../F-evolution/F01-selection/README.md)); more on levels in [A14](../A14-systems-thinking/README.md).
- A similar form for a similar function doesn't prove common descent: the wings of insects and birds are analogous, not homologous ([F02](../../F-evolution/F02-speciation/README.md)).
- **Trap:** naming a structure ("villi") isn't enough; say how its form serves the function ("villi enlarge the surface, so more nutrients are absorbed per unit of time").
