# A8 — Scientific Instruments: notes

These are English explanations of every learning outcome in [README.md](README.md), and every topic is *drafted*: written from the CCVX syllabus, the Dutch CvTE syllabus and standard VWO biology, not yet checked against an open source text or exam question.

- **In Dutch VWO:** central exam and school exam (CvTE §1.4), always inside a content question.
- **Dutch source:** [CvTE syllabus biologie vwo 2027](https://www.examenblad.nl/2027/vwo/documenten/syllabus-biologie-vwo-2027) (version 2, July 2025), domain A *Vaardigheden*, A8 *Natuurwetenschappelijk instrumentarium*. A citation like *(CvTE A8 spec 6)* means specification 6 of A8; the CvTE inserts a list of quantities and units as spec 3, so CCVX items 1–6 are CvTE specs 1, 2, 4, 5, 6 and 7.

Dutch terms are in brackets. Binas tables are named, not numbered, because the numbers differ between editions.

## 1. Obtaining and selecting information

- Getting information from written, spoken and audiovisual sources, partly with ICT; the ICT part is printed grey in the Dutch syllabus, and CCVX leaves out skills that need a computer. *(CvTE A8 spec 1; CCVX syllabus §1)*
- Extracting data from graphs, tables, drawings, simulations, charts and diagrams: read values with their units and check what each axis shows. In the oxygen saturation curve of haemoglobin, the saturation in the lungs minus that in working muscle is the share of O₂ released ([B03](../../B-self-regulation/B03-metabolism-in-organisms/README.md)).
- Looking up parameters, units, symbols, formulas and data in tables such as Binas: the Binas table of the genetic code (AUG codes for methionine and is the start codon, [B01](../../B-self-regulation/B01-protein-synthesis/README.md)), and the Binas tables of blood values, human hormones, and digestive juices and enzymes.
- **Trap:** reading the wrong curve, or missing a factor in an axis label such as "× 10³ cells per mL".

## 2. Analysing, presenting and organising data

- Analysing information and measurements and presenting them in graphs, drawings, charts, diagrams and tables, also with ICT. *(CvTE A8 spec 2)*
- Choose the form that fits: a line graph for a continuous variable, a bar chart for categories, a table for exact values, a diagram for a process such as a regulation cycle.
- The exam verb "draw" (*tekenen*) has rules: a graph has scaled axes, the independent variable on the x-axis, and quantities and units; a food web shows the organisms separately as far as possible, with arrows in the direction of the energy flow ([A02](../A02-communication/README.md)). *(CvTE appendix 5)*
- **Trap:** a food-web arrow points from the organism eaten to the one that eats it, not the other way round.

## 3. Techniques and apparatus

- Knowing which technique or apparatus measures which important quantity. *(CvTE A8 spec 4)*

| Quantity | Technique or apparatus | Unit |
|---|---|---|
| size of a cell | light microscope with a measuring scale | µm |
| volume of O₂ or CO₂ | gas syringe | mL |
| lung volumes | spirometer | L |
| blood pressure | blood pressure monitor | mmHg |
| pH | pH meter or indicator paper | none |
| membrane potential | microelectrode and oscilloscope | mV |
| length of DNA fragments | gel electrophoresis, after PCR or cutting with restriction enzymes | base pairs |

- Magnification is image size divided by real size: a cell 50 µm across that is 20 mm in a drawing is drawn at 20 mm / 0.050 mm = 400×.

## 4. Handling materials, instruments, organisms and the environment responsibly

- Working safely and carefully with materials, instruments, organisms and the environment: goggles and gloves with chemicals, sterile technique with microorganisms, and no release of cultures, exotic species or GMOs. Plates with cultures stay closed after incubation, because harmful bacteria may have grown on them. *(CvTE A8 spec 5)*
- Animal experiments follow the three Rs: replace animals where possible, reduce their number, and refine methods so that they suffer less.
- This item is printed grey in the Dutch syllabus: it is not tested in the central exam.

## 5. Arithmetical and mathematical skills

- The exam verbs "calculate" (*berekenen*) and "determine" (*bepalen*) require the values used and the steps, the right unit unless the question gives it, and rounding as asked. *(CvTE A8 spec 6; CvTE appendix 5)*
- Fractions and powers: 1 cm³ = 10⁻⁶ m³ = 1 mL; ten rounds of cell division turn one cell into 2¹⁰ = 1024 cells.
- Ratios, percentages and averages: a dihybrid cross AaBb × AaBb of unlinked genes with complete dominance gives 9 : 3 : 3 : 1, so 9/16 = 56.25% of the offspring show both dominant traits ([E03](../../E-reproduction/E03-reproduction-of-organisms/README.md)); the mean of 12, 14 and 13 mL is 13 mL.
- Area and volume: a cube of 1 cm has 6 cm² of surface per cm³, a cube of 2 cm only 24 / 8 = 3 cm² per cm³; for a sphere the ratio is 3/r, so smaller cells have more surface per volume. Villi, alveoli and root hairs enlarge the exchange surface ([B03](../../B-self-regulation/B03-metabolism-in-organisms/README.md)).
- Converting formulas: cardiac output = heart rate × stroke volume, so stroke volume = cardiac output / heart rate = 5.0 L min⁻¹ / 70 min⁻¹ = 71 mL.
- Drawing graphs: the independent variable on the x-axis and the dependent variable on the y-axis, each with quantity, unit and an even scale.
- Reading a logarithmic graph: each step on a log axis is a factor of 10, so halfway between 10³ and 10⁴ lies 10<sup>3.5</sup> ≈ 3.2·10³, not 5.5·10³. Bacterial counts are often plotted this way.
- Recognising linear (*lineair*) and exponential (*exponentieel*) relationships: a linear graph rises by the same amount per step, an exponential one by the same factor, and on a log y-axis exponential growth becomes a straight line ([A07](../A07-modelling/README.md)).
- Reading graphs without formulas: describe the trend in words, such as enzyme activity rising to an optimum temperature and then falling ([B02](../../B-self-regulation/B02-metabolism-in-cells/README.md)), or a population growing in an S-shaped curve ([C03](../../C-self-organization/C03-self-organization-of-ecosystems/README.md)).
- **Trap:** percentages aren't symmetric: from 400 to 500 is +25%, but from 500 back to 400 is −20%.

## 6. Relationships from tables and graphs

- Finding relationships in data: does one variable rise or fall with another, and which change comes first? After a meal blood glucose rises, insulin follows, and glucose then falls back ([B04](../../B-self-regulation/B04-self-regulation-of-organisms/README.md)). *(CvTE A8 spec 7)*
- **Trap:** a correlation in a table doesn't by itself show which variable causes which ([A05](../A05-research/README.md) item 11).

## CCVX versus the Dutch syllabus

- **In both:** CCVX items 1–6, as CvTE specs 1, 2, 4, 5, 6 and 7. The ICT part of item 1 and all of item 4 (CvTE spec 5) are printed grey, so they are not in the Dutch central exam. *(CvTE A8)*
- **Dutch syllabus only:** spec 3, the list of quantities and units with the prefixes from nano to giga, which CCVX partly lists under basic knowledge instead ([01-chemistry](../../0-basic-knowledge/01-chemistry/README.md), [02-physics](../../0-basic-knowledge/02-physics/README.md)); calculating with probabilities in spec 6, such as 1/4 for an aa child from Aa × Aa and 1/4 × 1/4 = 1/16 for two in a row ([E03](../../E-reproduction/E03-reproduction-of-organisms/README.md)); and spec 8, explaining how each variable in a formula affects the process, such as a thicker membrane slowing diffusion in Fick's law ([B03](../../B-self-regulation/B03-metabolism-in-organisms/README.md)).
- **CCVX only:** converting formulas and reading a logarithmic graph, which the CvTE doesn't list in spec 6.
