# A16 — Contexts: notes

These are English explanations of every learning outcome in [README.md](README.md), and every topic is *drafted*: written from the CCVX syllabus, the Dutch CvTE syllabus and standard VWO biology, not yet checked against an open source text or exam question.

- **In Dutch VWO:** central exam and school exam (CvTE §1.4), always inside a content question.
- **Dutch source:** [CvTE syllabus biologie vwo 2027](https://www.examenblad.nl/2027/vwo/documenten/syllabus-biologie-vwo-2027) (version 2, July 2025), domain A *Vaardigheden*, A16 *Contexten*. The CvTE splits the attainment target into three specifications that CCVX doesn't list; a citation like *(CvTE A16 spec 2)* means specification 2 of A16. The table in CvTE §1.4 swaps the names of A15 and A16, but the specifications and appendix 1 use A16 for contexts, as CCVX does.

Dutch terms are in brackets. Binas tables are named, not numbered, because the numbers differ between editions.

## What the skill means

- Using the skills of domain A and the concepts of domains B to F in at least three kinds of context: scientific, professional and everyday life. *(CvTE A16)*
- The CvTE context areas are energy, health or healthcare, sustainability, nutrition, food production, sport, world view and safety *(CvTE §2.2)*. The specimen contexts in the CCVX READMEs use the same labels and add communication.

## Three kinds of context

- Scientific contexts (*wetenschappelijke contexten*): research by scientists, such as evolutionary biologists at Naturalis building phylogenetic trees of plants from their traits and DNA ([F02](../../F-evolution/F02-speciation/README.md)). *(CvTE A16 spec 1)*
- Professional contexts (*beroepscontexten*): work that needs a scientific training, such as an optician advising on lenses ([B07](../../B-self-regulation/B07-perception-by-organisms/README.md)) or an exercise physiologist studying blood values ([B04](../../B-self-regulation/B04-self-regulation-of-organisms/README.md)); see [A04](../A04-studies-and-professions/README.md).
- Everyday-life contexts (*leefwereldcontexten*), which CCVX calls social environment contexts: a family deciding whether to take part in a study of a hereditary mitochondrial disorder ([B02](../../B-self-regulation/B02-metabolism-in-cells/README.md)) or a trial of an appetite inhibitor ([B03](../../B-self-regulation/B03-metabolism-in-organisms/README.md)), or forming an opinion from newspaper reports on antibiotics in livestock farming ([B05](../../B-self-regulation/B05-defence-in-organisms/README.md)).

## Same concept, different meanings

- A biological word can mean different things in different contexts. *(CvTE A16 spec 2)*
- Resistance: a bacterium's resistance (*resistentie*) to an antibiotic is a heritable property ([F02](../../F-evolution/F02-speciation/README.md)); a person's resistance (*weerstand*) to infection depends on the immune system ([B05](../../B-self-regulation/B05-defence-in-organisms/README.md)).
- Adaptation: in evolution, a heritable trait that raises fitness ([F02](../../F-evolution/F02-speciation/README.md)); in the senses, a receptor responding less to a stimulus that stays the same ([B07](../../B-self-regulation/B07-perception-by-organisms/README.md)). Fitness itself means reproductive success in evolution, but physical condition in sport.
- Pedigree: in genetics, a family tree showing a trait ([E03](../../E-reproduction/E03-reproduction-of-organisms/README.md)); in CCVX's "phylogenetic pedigree" in F02, a phylogenetic tree of species. Selection can be natural selection ([F02](../../F-evolution/F02-speciation/README.md)) or selection by breeders ([F01](../../F-evolution/F01-selection/README.md)).

## Linking concepts to the context

- Relate the terms in the context to biological concepts; a context may introduce new terms, which it then explains itself. *(CvTE A16 spec 3; CvTE §2.3)*
- Example: an athlete trains at altitude (context): the thinner air holds less O₂ per litre, the kidneys release more EPO, the bone marrow makes more red blood cells, and back at sea level the blood carries more O₂ ([B04](../../B-self-regulation/B04-self-regulation-of-organisms/README.md)).
- **Trap:** a general textbook answer that ignores the context, or a summary of the context that never names the biological concept, both miss what is asked.
