### bio-mock1-q01 | points 2 | outcome B1.1.2 | difficulty medium

#### Question

Spider silk is extremely strong. Bacteria with a recombinant plasmid can produce a spider-silk protein. The gene used for this was made as cDNA from mRNA of the silk gland of an orb-weaver spider.

Part of the mRNA that is translated reads:

5'-AUG GGU GCA GGU GGU GCA UAG-3'

Genetic code (from the BINAS table): AUG Met, GGU Gly, GCA Ala, AGC Ser, UAG stop, UGA stop.

The researchers used cDNA, not the gene as it lies in the spider's chromosomes. Explain why bacteria can express the cDNA correctly but not the chromosomal gene.

#### Answer

The chromosomal gene contains introns, and bacteria cannot splice (cannot remove introns from pre-mRNA).

#### Solution

The chromosomal gene contains introns, and bacteria cannot splice (cannot remove introns from pre-mRNA). cDNA is copied from mature mRNA, so it consists of exons only and is translated correctly.
*Marking:* • chromosomal gene has introns; bacteria cannot splice (1) • cDNA = exons only, from mature mRNA (1)

### bio-mock1-q07 | points 2 | outcome B2.2.3 | difficulty medium

#### Question

During long efforts an athlete loses water and salts in sweat. A sports drink is "isotonic" when the total concentration of dissolved particles equals that of blood plasma, so the drink neither holds water back in the intestine nor draws water into it. Blood plasma has an osmotic value of about 300 mmol of dissolved particles per litre.

A sports drink contains, per litre, 50 mmol glucose and 100 mmol NaCl. Glucose dissolves as molecules; each NaCl gives two ions, Na⁺ and Cl⁻.

Show with a calculation whether this sports drink is isotonic with blood plasma.

#### Answer

Dissolved particles: glucose 50 mmol (molecules) + NaCl 100 × 2 = 200 mmol (ions) → 250 mmol L⁻¹.

#### Solution

Dissolved particles: glucose 50 mmol (molecules) + NaCl 100 × 2 = 200 mmol (ions) → 250 mmol L⁻¹. That is less than 300 mmol L⁻¹, so the drink is not isotonic but slightly hypotonic.
*Marking:* • ×2 for the two ions (1) • 250 < 300 with conclusion (1)

*Marking:* • survival advantage for worms with d under treatment (1) • medicine = selection pressure → d rises (1)
