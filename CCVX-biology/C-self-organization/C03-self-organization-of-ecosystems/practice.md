In 2020 a storm fells several old oak trees. A student claims: "In those open spots the succession has to start all over again." Assess this claim using the term climax ecosystem and your knowledge of dynamics.

#### Answer

The claim is wrong.

#### Solution

The claim is wrong. A climax ecosystem (here the oak woodland) is a dynamic equilibrium, not a static one: storms and diseases kill trees all the time and create gaps. In a gap there is still soil, a rich seed bank and root systems, so the gap is refilled by local (secondary) succession: light-demanding species and fast-growing saplings take it first, and the woodland closes again within decades. Disturbance gives a living mosaic of stages rather than a restart of the whole succession.
*Marking:* • claim rejected (1) • climax is dynamic / disturbances create gaps, soil and seeds remain (1) • gap refilled by local (secondary) succession; mosaic of stages (1)
