# CCVX Biology — Mock exam 2 (original)

**Time: 3 hours · 8 questions · 78 points**

This is an original mock exam in the style of the CCVX preliminary examination in biology: open questions in contexts, with the points per sub-question shown. All contexts, data and questions are invented for practice; nothing is copied from CCVX or CvTE exams.

**Allowed:** BINAS or Biodata (5th edition or later, without notes); a non-graphing calculator with logarithms; one paper dictionary. Where a question needs a data table, the data are given within the sub-question.

Answers and the marking scheme are in [mock-exam-2-marking-scheme.md](mock-exam-2-marking-scheme.md). Item ids run continuously (bio-mock2-q01 … q38) across the eight exam questions.

### bio-mock2-q01 | points 2 | outcome E2.3 | difficulty easy

#### Question

At a dermatology research centre, technicians culture keratinocytes (skin cells) from a small skin biopsy. In a well-fed culture the cells divide regularly. A mole removed from the same patient turns out to contain a tumour whose cells divide much faster and without the normal restraints.

#### Question

- mean speed of impulse conduction in the myelinated neurons concerned: 80 m s⁻¹;

Show with a calculation that the 0.100 s limit is consistent with these data, and argue why a runner who moves 0.080 s after the shot cannot have reacted to the sound.
