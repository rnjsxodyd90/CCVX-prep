# CCVX Biology

Study material for the [CCVX](https://www.ccvx.nl/index%20-%20EN.html) entrance exam (*voortentamen*) in biology, organised by the CCVX syllabus in `syllabus/ccvx-syllabus-biology-EN.pdf` (dated 21 September 2017). It is based on the Dutch VWO biology programme from May 2016 and covers both the central-exam and the school-exam parts. Domains B–F are tested together with the skills in domain A, except what can't be tested in writing. Basic chemistry and physics are assumed, but never tested on their own. Where the CCVX syllabus and the current Dutch syllabus differ, the CCVX syllabus applies.

## The exam

- Written, 3 hours, in Dutch or English, in Utrecht only. Registration closes 10 working days before the exam ([conditions](https://www.ccvx.nl/Inschrijving/VoorwaardenDeelname.html), article 4), so Thursday 19 November 2026 for the December exam; the fee is €98 and can't be refunded.
- Candidates whose native language isn't Dutch can ask for extra time, with a birth certificate as proof, by the same deadline ([conditions](https://www.ccvx.nl/Inschrijving/VoorwaardenDeelname.html), article 5).
- Dates in 2026/27: Thursday 3 December 2026 (13:30–16:30), around 14 April 2027 and around 14 July 2027.
- Allowed: Binas, 5th edition or later, or Biodata instead, with no notes in it; a non-graphing calculator with logarithmic functions; one paper dictionary, either a one-volume Dutch dictionary or a Dutch-to-other-language one (no digital dictionaries) ([exam requirements](https://www.ccvx.nl/Tentamenbenodigdheden%20en%20Handelingswerkwoorden.html)).
- Bring: valid ID (passport, Dutch ID card or driving licence, EU/EEA document, or Dutch residence permit), a pen (not red; pencil only for graphs), a set square (*geodriehoek*), a watch (no smartwatch; a phone can't be used as a clock) and spare calculator batteries. Only one calculator is allowed.
- CCVX has one [example exam in English](https://www.ccvx.nl/VoorbeeldTentamensEngels/Voorbeeldtentamen%20biologie%20-%20EN.pdf), with [worked answers in Dutch](https://www.ccvx.nl/VoorbeeldTentamens/Voorbeeldtentamen%20biologie%20-%20Uitwerking.pdf).

## Layout

| Folder | Syllabus |
|---|---|
| `0-basic-knowledge/` | assumed chemistry and physics |
| `A-skills/` | A1–A16 |
| `B-self-regulation/` | B1–B8 |
| `C-self-organization/` | C1–C3 |
| `D-interaction/` | D1–D5 |
| `E-reproduction/` | E1–E3 |
| `F-evolution/` | F1–F4 |
| `syllabus/` | the CCVX syllabus PDF |

Each domain folder's `README.md` has the domain's general outcomes. Each subfolder's `README.md` has that subdomain's outcomes, specimen contexts and specific concepts, as printed in the PDF with formulas and obvious typos fixed. Subfolder numbers are zero-padded so they sort: `B01` is syllabus B1.

Unlike [CCVX-chemistry](../CCVX-chemistry/), these folders don't yet say where each topic sits in the Dutch syllabus, and there's no plan for filling them.
