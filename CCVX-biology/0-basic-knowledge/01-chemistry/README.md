# Basic Knowledge of Chemistry

This basic knowledge is not tested directly or in isolation from a biological case in the Preliminary Examination.

The following topics are assumed to be known for biology:

- Atom, molecule, ion, proton
- Atomic mass
- Molecular formula, structural formula
- Reagents
- Reaction equation, equilibrium reactions, catalyst
- Water-soluble or fat-soluble (hydrophilic, hydrophobic), saturation
- Acids and bases, pH, indicators
- Reducing agent and oxidizing agent
- Amino acids, proteins, peptide binding
- Fats, glycerol, saturated/unsaturated fatty acids
- Carbohydrates: monosaccharides, disaccharides and polysaccharides
- Hydrogen and sulfur bridges
- Methane, alcohol (ethanol)
- Names and formulas of the following substances: ammonia, calcium, iron, potassium, carbon dioxide, magnesium, sodium chloride, nitrogen and water
- Quantities and units: concentration (mol/L or g/L), percentage by mass, percentage by volume, ppm.
