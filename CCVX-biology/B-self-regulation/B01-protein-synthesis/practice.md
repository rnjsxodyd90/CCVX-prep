### bio-b01-q02 | points 2 | outcome B1.1.1 | difficulty medium

#### Question

Biologists isolate DNA from a bacterium that lives in hot springs at about 75 °C. Analysis shows that 37% of its bases are cytosine.

A second bacterial species, from pond water at 20 °C, has DNA of which 26% of the bases are cytosine. Explain which of the two DNA samples will separate into single strands at the lower temperature when heated.

#### Answer

The pond-water DNA separates at the lower temperature.

#### Solution

The pond-water DNA separates at the lower temperature. It has fewer G–C pairs (52%) than the hot-spring DNA (74%); G–C pairs are held by three hydrogen bonds and A–T pairs by two, so DNA richer in G–C needs more heat (energy) to separate.
*Marking:* • correct choice, pond-water DNA (1) • G–C has three hydrogen bonds, A–T two, so more energy is needed with more G–C (1)

### bio-b01-q09 | points 3 | outcome B1.1.3 | difficulty easy

#### Question

A forensic laboratory finds a tiny blood stain. For the DNA profile, one region of 400 base pairs is multiplied with the polymerase chain reaction (PCR). At the start there is exactly one double-stranded DNA molecule of the region, plus an excess of primers, free DNA nucleotides and Taq polymerase.

PCR repeats one cycle of three temperature steps. Name these three steps and describe what happens to the DNA or the primers in each step.

#### Answer

Denaturation, about 95 °C: the strands separate (hydrogen bonds break). Annealing, about 50–65 °C: the primers pair with their complementary sequences on both strands. Extension, about 72 °C: Taq polymerase builds the new strands from the primers with free nucleotides.

#### Solution

Denaturation, about 95 °C: the strands separate (hydrogen bonds break). Annealing, about 50–65 °C: the primers pair with their complementary sequences on both strands. Extension, about 72 °C: Taq polymerase builds the new strands from the primers with free nucleotides.
*Marking:* • denaturation with strand separation at high temperature (1) • annealing = primer binding at lowered temperature (1) • extension = synthesis by (Taq) polymerase (1)
