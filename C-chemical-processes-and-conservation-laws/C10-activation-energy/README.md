# C10 — Activation Energy

- **In Dutch VWO:** school exam (SE) only, so it isn't in the CvTE syllabus. CCVX still tests it.
- **Dutch source:** [SLO handreiking SE (vwo)](https://www.slo.nl/handreikingen/havo-vwo/handreiking-se-schei-hv/toetsen-schoolexamen/vakinhoudelijke-0/) → C10. Overlap: [CvTE 2027](../../syllabus/cvte-syllabus-scheikunde-vwo-2027.pdf) R3.2, R5.1 and L2.1 (enzymes).

## Learning outcomes (CCVX syllabus)

The candidate is able to use and describe the concept of activation energy in experiments and relate it to catalysis:

- activation energy
- catalyst
- enzymes
- energy diagram, element conservation.
